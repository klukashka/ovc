**Translation.** This is an English translation of the Russian user agreement. The Russian text is the binding version.

**1. Parties and acceptance.**

- The agreement is made between the individual entrepreneur [full name], OGRNIP [OGRNIP], INN [INN] (the operator), and a user of the OVC service (the service).
- The agreement is a public offer of the free use of the service. A user accepts it by pressing the button that creates the account, next to which the agreement is linked. Ticking the consent to the processing of personal data does not accept the agreement. [verify: articles 428, 435, 437 and 438 of the Civil Code and whether this form of acceptance suffices]

**2. Subject.**

- The operator provides free access to the service for tutors teaching students: video lessons, chats, the schedule and homework.
- The service is not an educational organisation; the tutor teaches. [verify: the wording on the service's status]

**3. Accounts.**

- One account belongs to one person. A user gives accurate data and keeps the password secret.
- The service has two roles: tutor and student.

**4. The tutor's obligations.**

- A tutor warrants that each student agreed to be invited and to the data the tutor enters about them.
- A tutor keeps students' data in the service, enters no more than needed and puts no special categories of personal data in notes and chats without need.
- Notes about a student are the student's personal data; the operator may disclose them to the student on a lawful request.
- Content a tutor posts must be lawful.

**5. The student's position.**

- One student account may study with several tutors. Each tutor sees only their own lessons with the student.
- Only the tutor sees points and scores for work.

**6. Content rules.**

- Unlawful content, insults, harassment and unsolicited messages are prohibited.
- Complaints go to [email address for requests]. The operator may block an account or remove content that breaks the agreement.

**7. Uploaded materials.**

- Users keep their rights in materials. A user grants the operator the right to store the materials and show them to the participants they are meant for.
- A user is responsible for third parties' rights in uploaded materials.

**8. Data exports.**

- A user who downloads an export of their data is responsible for the files. A tutor uses students' data from an export only for teaching them, protects it and deletes it when the purpose is reached. [verify: whether this obligation is enforceable for a tutor who is a user]

**9. Lessons.**

- A video lesson's participants are the tutor and the students the lesson is for. Lessons take place live and are not recorded. [How lessons are recorded, who sees recordings, their retention and how participants are told will be described when recordings exist.]

**10. Deleting an account and ending lessons.**

- A user may delete their account in the settings. Deletion and ending lessons have the effects the service describes: for example, students of a deleted tutor can read their results for 30 days.

**11. Availability and liability.**

- The service is provided as is. The operator's liability is limited as far as the law allows. [verify: limits of liability for a free service under consumer protection law]
