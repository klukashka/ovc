**Translation.** This is an English translation of the Russian privacy policy. The Russian text is the binding version.

**1. General provisions.**

- This policy describes how the individual entrepreneur [full name], OGRNIP [OGRNIP], INN [INN], address [address] (the operator) processes the personal data of the users of the OVC service (the service).
- The operator is the only operator of all personal data in the service, including the data tutors enter about their students. A tutor is a user of the service, not an operator, and the service does not process data on a tutor's behalf.
- The current edition of the policy is always available on the service's privacy policy page. Its version and date are shown above the text.
- The policy is issued under item 2 of part 1 and part 2 of article 18.1 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data". [verify: the edition of article 18.1 and the requirements for publishing the policy on a website]

**2. Terms.**

- Personal data, processing, operator and processor have the meaning Federal Law No. 152-FZ gives them.
- A tutor is a user who teaches, creates student cards, invites students and issues work.
- A student is a user who studies with one or more tutors.

**3. Legal grounds.**

- The consent of the data subject (item 1 of part 1 of article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ), given at sign-up.
- The user agreement, to which the user is a party (item 5 of part 1 of article 6).
- For the name and email a tutor enters before a student signs up, the legitimate interests of the operator and the tutor (item 7 of part 1 of article 6), supported by the tutor's warranty in the user agreement that the student agreed to be invited. [verify: whether this ground applies and the balance of interests]

**4. Purposes and data.**

- Accounts and sign-in: name, email, password hash, profile photo, language, timezone, sessions; against password guessing, keyed hashes of IP addresses and emails in attempt counters. Kept until the account is deleted and 30 days more, then deleted; unconfirmed accounts 7 days; an account the operator blocked until the operator unblocks, restores or deletes it, with no bound.
- Expired and revoked sessions are deleted after a day; email requests (address confirmation, password reset) and their links 7 days after the request.
- Student cards and invitations: the card's name, email and the tutor's notes. These data come from the tutor, not from the student (part 3 of article 18). An unaccepted card's email is erased after 90 days; withdrawing an invitation or merging cards erases the name, email and notes at once; removing an unconfirmed or pending link erases its name, and the email and notes move to a new card. The retention of an unaccepted card's name and notes, of an ended card's name, email and notes (kept until the student's or the tutor's final deletion) and of the card left after the deletion of a student account with an unconfirmed link: [period to be set]. After a student's account is deleted, the name in the tutor's records is replaced with a fixed label and the notes are erased.
- Relationships with tutors, tutor-code requests and blocked requesters, groups, schedule and lessons.
- Video lessons on the conferencing server: from the service, the display name, an opaque identifier, the role and the lesson's presentation file; from participants' browsers, IP addresses, audio, video and screen, and the room's chat and whiteboard, live and not recorded. The conferencing server's logs are kept up to 14 days. The presentation file's retention on the conferencing server: [period to be set].
- Chats, files and chat presence. A deleted user's messages are shown from a "Deleted user" for the 30 days before the final deletion. The chat of a discarded link (removed, dropped or expired) is kept until its final erasure, which has no fixed deadline. [verify: whether a period without a fixed bound is acceptable]
- Homework, answers and reviews: answers and reviews published to the student, points and scores visible only to the tutor. Work stays readable after the lessons end.
- Service email, email preferences and undeliverable marks from the mail provider; the stream of undeliverable events is kept for the shortest period the provider allows. In-app notifications are kept 90 days.
- Administration: account blocking and the operator's audit trail.
- The export of a user's own data on request: the archive holds what the user can read in the service, including other people's data visible to them. It is kept at most 3 days and deleted earlier when data in it stop being available in the service. [verify: whether the export changes the purpose and needs a mention in the consent]
- Logs and security, including minimal records of unfinished file cleanup; database logs are kept up to 14 days.
- The retention of the reverse proxy's logs, the operator's audit trail and the application's diagnostic logs: [to be set in the deployment plan].
