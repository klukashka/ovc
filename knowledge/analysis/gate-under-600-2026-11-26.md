# End-to-end consolidation to bring the merge gate under 600 s (2026-11-26)

Recorded for the change `chore/gate-under-600`, which the owner approved on 2026-11-26. The goal is a merge gate median under 600 s without losing coverage; ADR-0018's consolidation threshold is 540 s.

Measured before the change, from the gate logs, with nothing run:

- Lane A (`go test -race -count=1 ./...`) is set by `internal/api`, and earlier records rejected every lever that keeps one database per test.
- The end-to-end phase is close to the summed test time divided by the 6 workers, plus about 40 s outside the tests.

The change is therefore consolidation only:

- merges of tests with the same arrangement and action;
- removal of exact duplicates;
- product timers advanced with Playwright's page clock;
- seeds sent in bounded batches.

It follows the earlier consolidation records `e2e-small-consolidation-2026-11-12.md` and `e2e-consolidation-2026-11-16.md`, and does not repeat what they rejected:

- Chromium-only subsets;
- worker-scoped shared arrangements;
- action prefixes merged into the arrangement of another test.

Every test that leaves names the tests that still hold each of its assertions.

## Timers advanced with the page clock

Every use is opted in per test. The clock is installed before the test's first navigation, without a fixed time, so the page's time keeps flowing. The only calls used are `install`, `runFor` and `fastForward`, which ADR-0009 item 13 lists. Each advance follows the event that arms the timer and is followed by a positive assertion. An advance that lands before its timer is armed does not fire that timer, and the test then waits for it in real time as before. The `docs/development.md` clock line was amended for the three non-save timers and the notes save (pending owner review).

## Results

All runs went through the main checkout's `tools/dev-env.sh`, with `--trace=off`, at a load below 6 for both the 1- and 5-minute averages, on AC power.

- **Test list.** The list was taken from the full runs' reports, on the base `512765d` and on the branch. Per engine it differs by 16 removed tests plus 8 survivor renames, where the old title goes out and the new one comes in. That is 24 titles out and 8 in per engine, and 72 out and 24 in over the Chromium, Firefox and WebKit projects. No `pilot-` title changed.
- **Product breaks.** Every holder that gained an assertion was given a temporary product edit, run once in Chromium. Each passed without the edit and failed with it, on the gained assertion's line. Rows 3, 6 and 10 of the plan used a different edit than planned, because the planned edit would have failed an earlier assertion or not taken effect. Each edit was reverted by editing the file back, and `git diff` of the product was empty afterwards. The 360 px markup sweeps were not broken, because an injected-markup break needs a raw-HTML sink that the frontend lint forbids. The sweep assertion holds them, as argued above.
- **Touched specs.** They ran in all three engines with `--repeat-each=3` and `--retries=0`, the pilot step tests excluded: 378 passed. `signup.spec.ts` "pilot-1 › steps 1–3" ran separately in all three engines and passed.
- **Desktop layout after the title check of the 360 px sweeps.** `desktop-layout.spec.ts` ran in all three engines: 78 passed.
- **Full runs, base `512765d` against the branch on it,** both with `--trace=off`, one after the other on an otherwise idle machine:

| Run | Tests | Chromium | Firefox | WebKit | Summed | Wall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base `512765d` | 2628 passed | 548.8 s | 1271.3 s | 827.1 s | 2647.3 s | 490.8 s |
| branch | 2580 passed | 503.2 s | 990.9 s | 731.6 s | 2225.6 s | 413.9 s |

The summed time fell by 421.7 s. The specs this change does not touch also fell, by 110.4 s, from 868.1 s to 757.7 s, which is run-to-run noise. Scaling the base's touched specs by the untouched ratio (0.873) gives about 85 s for the change itself. That is above the 70 s threshold, though with little margin. An earlier branch run, on the base `db28015` with a local development server running, summed 2393.8 s. Nearly all of the change's gain comes from the removed tests; the page-clock advances save about 45 s summed, and the seed batching shows no measurable gain. Some advances may land before their timer is armed and then wait in real time, so they are not credited with the full second.
