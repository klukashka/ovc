# Taxes, payments and legal services for a small Russian subscription service: facts checked on 2026-11-23

Checked 2026-11-23. This file is agent research: it decides nothing and is not tax or legal advice. It updates and extends [taxes and acquiring, 2026-10-25](taxes-and-acquiring-2026-10-25.md) and the payments section of [cost levers and market, 2026-10-25](cost-levers-and-market-2026-10-25.md#payments-for-a-tutor-service) for a service in which tutors pay a flat monthly fee of roughly 990–1,490 ₽, students pay nothing, annual revenue is between zero and roughly 4–8 million ₽, and the operator is or will be an individual entrepreneur (ИП). Rates, limits, tariffs and prices change; confirm them with an accountant, the tax service and the payment provider before relying on them.

## Method and conventions

- Primary sources are preferred: the text of laws on consultant.ru, garant.ru and legalacts.ru, the tax service (nalog.gov.ru), the Bank of Russia (cbr.ru) and providers' own tariff pages. A consultant.ru or garant.ru news item about a law is treated as primary for the law's existence, number and date.
- Figures taken from accounting portals, banks' blogs, aggregators and reviews are marked **third-party**. Facts no reachable source confirmed are marked **unconfirmed**.
- Several bank sites (tbank.ru, tochka.com, sberbank.com) could not be read on 2026-11-23 because of a TLS certificate error on the research machine, so their figures come from aggregators.

## Changes since the 2026-10-25 files

- **VAT threshold of the simplified system stays at 20 million ₽ for 2026–2029.** Federal Law No. 228-FZ of 2026-07-04, in force the same day, cancelled the planned cuts to 15 million ₽ in 2027 and 10 million ₽ in 2028 ([ConsultantPlus news, 2026-07-04](https://www.consultant.ru/legalnews/31982/); [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/hotlaw/federal/2154941/)). The later steps are 15 million ₽ and then 10 million ₽; third-party sources date them to 2030 and 2031 ([Finance Mail.ru, 2026-09-10](https://finance.mail.ru/article/kogda-biznesu-na-usn-mozhno-ne-platit-nds-v-2027-godu-69226446/), **third-party**). Garant's page is partly garbled, and its readable fragment can also be read as "20 million ₽ of income for 2025–2028, 15 million ₽ for 2029, 10 million ₽ for 2030", which gives the same calendar years. The 2027–2028 dates are **unconfirmed** against the consolidated text of article 145. The tax service's February 2026 page still shows the old 20/15/10 schedule and is marked archived ([nalog.gov.ru, 2026-02-10](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn72/news/tax_doc_news/16601719/)).
- **Card acquiring now carries 22% VAT.** From 2026-01-01, Federal Law No. 425-FZ of 2025-11-28 removed the VAT exemption for services connected with bank-card operations. Payments through the Faster Payments System (СБП) stay exempt ([ppt.ru on the Ministry of Finance letter of 2025-12-24 No. 03-07-05/126006](https://ppt.ru/obzory/minfin/pozitsiya-minfina-rf-o-tom-kakie-operatsii-s-bankovskimi-kartami-s-2026-goda-oblagayutsya-nds-a-kakie-net), [Yandex Pay blog](https://pay.yandex.ru/blog/articles/ekvajring-2026), **third-party**). An ИП on the simplified system cannot deduct that VAT, so a card fee quoted "without VAT" costs 1.22 times as much.
- **Fixed contributions for 2027 are now known**: 61,154 ₽, and the 1% part is capped at 342,923 ₽ ([art. 430 of the Tax Code, legalacts.ru](https://legalacts.ru/kodeks/NK-RF-chast-2/razdel-xi/glava-34/statja-430/), [Kontur.Elba](https://kontur.ru/elba/spravka/55378-straxovye_vznosy_ip_gajd_po_oplate_i_raschetam), **third-party**).
- **The 2027 income limit of the simplified system rises to 518.85 million ₽.** This is 450 million ₽ × a deflator of 1.153. A source reports that the Ministry of Economic Development's order No. 732 took effect on 2026-11-10 ([Bitrix24 journal](https://www.bitrix24.ru/journal/news/limity-usn-2027-koeffitsient-deflyator/), **third-party**); another source still calls it a draft ([Moe Delo](https://www.moedelo.org/club/article-knowledge/koefficienty-deflatory-na-2027-god-kak-izmenatsa-limity-dla-usn-patenta-i-nds), **third-party**).
- **Acquiring rates are now confirmed on providers' own pages** (YooKassa, Robokassa), replacing the October file's "from 0.4%" indication. The figures are in the acquiring section below.

## 1. Professional income tax (НПД), including an ИП on НПД

- **Term and law.** The regime is an experiment under Federal Law No. 422-FZ of 2018-11-27 that runs until 2028-12-31 inclusive. The consolidated text is current to 2026-08-04 (amending law No. 285-FZ) ([422-FZ, ConsultantPlus](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_311977/)).
- **Rates and deduction.** The rate is 4% on income from individuals and 6% on income from legal entities and ИП. A one-time tax deduction of 10,000 ₽ lowers the effective rate to 3% and 4% until it is used up ([Kontur](https://kontur.ru/articles/5337), [Mail.ru Finance](https://finance.mail.ru/card/npd-961/), **third-party**; rates are in art. 10 of 422-FZ, which was not re-read here). For this service the payers are tutors. A tutor who pays as a private person is charged 4%, and a tutor who pays from an ИП account is charged 6%. Treating a self-employed tutor who pays by card as an individual for the 4% rate is **unconfirmed**.
- **Limit.** The regime is not available once income counted for the tax exceeds 2.4 million ₽ in the current calendar year (art. 4 part 2 item 8). The limit is unchanged in the 2026-08-04 text ([art. 4 of 422-FZ](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_311977/30ba0412dfedfe46e8061ea063246bac59da2b69/)). It is not indexed.
- **No employees.** A person with workers under employment contracts cannot apply the regime (art. 4 part 2 item 4). Contractors under civil-law contracts are allowed.
- **Exclusions, art. 4 part 2 of 422-FZ** ([text](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_311977/30ba0412dfedfe46e8061ea063246bac59da2b69/)):
  1. selling excisable goods and goods subject to mandatory marking;
  2. reselling goods or property rights, except property used for personal needs;
  3. extracting or selling minerals;
  4. having employees;
  5. business in another person's interest under mandate, commission or agency contracts, except in the cases of part 6;
  6. delivering goods and accepting payment for them in another person's interest, unless the seller's cash register is used;
  7. combining with another special regime;
  8. exceeding the 2.4 million ₽ limit;
  9. mining and trading digital currency.

  Art. 6 part 2 also takes some income out of the regime: employment income, income from a current employer or one from the last two years, assignment of claims, income in kind, sales of real estate, vehicles and securities, and others ([art. 6 of 422-FZ](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_311977/27164c06aa904117df15dede95fb55764e9054e7/)).
- **Selling access to one's own online service.** No law or letter found names a SaaS subscription. The Ministry of Finance has written that income from selling rights to one's own results of intellectual activity may be taxed under the regime if the general restrictions are met (letter of 2023-06-09 No. 03-11-11/53431, reported by [buh.ru](https://buh.ru/news/uchet_nalogi/169843/), **third-party**). It has also written that income from **reselling** software may not be taxed under the regime (letter of 2022-03-15 No. 03-11-11/19322, reported by [v2b.ru](https://www.v2b.ru/2022/04/05/pri-pereprodazhe-programmnogo-obespecheniya-nelzya-primenyat-npd/), **third-party**). A subscription to software the operator wrote and runs fits the "own result" reading and none of the art. 4 exclusions. Two points stay **unconfirmed**: that a subscription fits, and that collecting tutors' fees is not an agency arrangement. It would become agency if the service collected students' payments on tutors' behalf.
- **Insurance contributions.** An ИП on НПД does not pay the fixed contributions for the periods under the regime (art. 430 para. 11 of the Tax Code, [legalacts.ru](https://legalacts.ru/kodeks/NK-RF-chast-2/razdel-xi/glava-34/statja-430/)). Pension contributions are voluntary. From 2026 a voluntary sick-leave insurance experiment runs until 2028: 3.84% of a chosen insured sum of 35,000 or 50,000 ₽, or about 1,344 or 1,920 ₽ a month, with benefits after 6 months of payments ([Kontur](https://kontur.ru/articles/5293), [SFR Tver branch](https://sfr.gov.ru/branches/tver/info/~/14932), **third-party** for the amounts).
- **Receipts and cash registers.** Receipts are issued in the tax service's «Мой налог» app or through a partner provider's integration. Income taxed under the regime does not require a cash register under 54-FZ (422-FZ and 54-FZ as described by [Robokassa](https://robokassa.com/payments/tarify/), **third-party**). YooKassa stopped issuing self-employed receipts for payments and refunds on 2025-12-29 ([Quasa](https://quasa.io/ru/media/yukassa-ili-robokassa-dlya-samozanyatogo-komissii-cheki-i-usloviya-podklyucheniya), **third-party**). Robokassa says its "Робочеки СМЗ" receipts for the self-employed are included in its tariff ([Robokassa blog, 2026-07-21](https://robokassa.com/blog/news/kak-snizit-komissiyu-v-robokassa-napominaem-pro-tarify/)).
- **Exceeding the limit mid-year.** The right ends with the receipt that takes income past 2.4 million ₽, and the tax service removes the person from the regime automatically. Under part 6 of art. 15 of 422-FZ, an ИП may notify a move to the simplified system (or the agricultural tax, and from 2025 the patent or the automated simplified system) within 20 calendar days. Sources differ on when the 20 days start: at the loss of the right, or at removal from the regime. The simplified system then applies from the date of removal. Without a notice the ИП falls under the general regime (personal income tax 13–15% and VAT) until the year ends, and can switch to the simplified system only from 1 January ([Tochka](https://allo.tochka.com/limity-samozaniatyx), [Kontur](https://kontur.ru/articles/38088-perexod_iz_samozanyatogo_v_ip), [ConsultantPlus selection](https://www.consultant.ru/law/podborki/samozanyatyj_prevysil_limit/), **third-party**).

## 2. Simplified system on income (УСН «доходы») for an ИП in 2026 and 2027

- **Rate.** The rate is 6% on income. A region may lower it to as little as 1% for chosen activities or categories (art. 346.20 para. 1 of the Tax Code; general rule, not re-read here). The rate in the operator's region is **unconfirmed**.
- **Fixed contributions** (art. 430 of the Tax Code, [legalacts.ru](https://legalacts.ru/kodeks/NK-RF-chast-2/razdel-xi/glava-34/statja-430/); [nalog.gov.ru for 2026](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn53/news/activities_fts/16606867/)):
  - 2026: 57,390 ₽ due by 2026-12-28, plus 1% of income above 300,000 ₽, capped at 321,818 ₽ and due by 2027-07-01;
  - 2027: 61,154 ₽, with the 1% part capped at 342,923 ₽ (**third-party** reading of art. 430; the cap matches the art. 430 figures quoted by several sources).
  - At 4 million ₽ of income in 2026, the 1% part is 37,000 ₽ and contributions total 94,390 ₽. At 8 million ₽ the 1% part is 77,000 ₽ and the total is 134,390 ₽.
- **Reducing the tax.** An ИП without employees reduces the 6% tax by 100% of the contributions paid for themself, the 1% part included. An ИП with employees reduces it by at most 50% (art. 346.21 para. 3.1 of the Tax Code; general rule as described by [Kontur.Elba](https://kontur.ru/elba/spravka/55378-straxovye_vznosy_ip_gajd_po_oplate_i_raschetam), **third-party**). Below roughly 1 million ₽ of income a year, the tax is usually reduced to zero and only the contributions are paid.
- **Income limit of the regime.** 2026: 490.5 million ₽ (450 million ₽ × 1.090). 2027: 518.85 million ₽ (× 1.153), if the order on the deflator stands ([tax service brochure](https://data.nalog.ru/files/media/usn_01012026.pdf), [Bitrix24 journal](https://www.bitrix24.ru/journal/news/limity-usn-2027-koeffitsient-deflyator/), **third-party** for 2027). This limit is far above the service's revenue.
- **VAT on the simplified system.** A payer is exempt while income for the previous year and the current year stays within 20 million ₽ in 2026–2029, as Federal Law No. 228-FZ of 2026-07-04 now sets it (see the changes above). The exemption is automatic and needs no notice. Above the threshold, VAT applies from the first day of the month after the month in which it was exceeded. The payer then chooses one of two options:
  - a special rate of 5%, for 2026 income up to 272.5 million ₽, or 7%, up to 490.5 million ₽, both without input VAT deductions (indexed: about 288.25 million ₽ for the 5% band in 2027);
  - the general rates with deductions ([Tochka](https://allo.tochka.com/usn-izmeneniy), [Moe Delo](https://www.moedelo.org/club/article-knowledge/koefficienty-deflatory-na-2027-god-kak-izmenatsa-limity-dla-usn-patenta-i-nds), **third-party**).
  At 4–8 million ₽ of revenue the threshold does not apply.
- **General VAT rate.** The rate is 22% from 2026-01-01 under Federal Law No. 425-FZ of 2025-11-28 ([nalog.gov.ru](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn26/news/smi/16596761/); tax service letter of 2025-12-29 No. СД-4-3/11802@ on [ConsultantPlus](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_523620/)).
- **VAT exemption for software.** Sub-paragraph 26 of paragraph 2 of article 149 exempts the transfer of rights to programs and databases, remote access over the internet included. Since 2025 the exemption covers only software listed in the unified register of Russian programs ([ConsultantPlus selection](https://www.consultant.ru/law/podborki/osvobozhdenie_ot_nds_programmnoe_obespechenie/), [ppt.ru](https://ppt.ru/art/nds/osvobozhdenie-ot-nds-dlya-it-kompaniy), **third-party**). A 2025 bill (No. 1026190-8) proposed abolishing the exemption from 2026. Later third-party material describes the exemption as still in force and cites a Ministry of Finance letter of 2026-05-18 No. 03-07-07/41161 on applying it to SaaS (**unconfirmed**). The exemption matters only above the 20 million ₽ threshold, and only if the service is entered in the register.

## 3. Automated simplified system (АУСН)

The automated simplified system charges 8% on income, or 20% on income minus expenses with a 3% minimum. Its tax replaces VAT, personal income tax and the ИП's own insurance contributions, leaving only an injury contribution of about 2,750 ₽ a year per employer with staff. It requires income up to 60 million ₽ a year, at most 5 employees, cashless settlement only, and an account at an authorized bank, and it has no declarations. It was extended from the original four regions to most of the country from 2026-01-01; sources report 83 regions. It applies only where the region has adopted a law and runs until 2027-12-31 under Federal Law No. 17-FZ of 2022-02-25 ([Kontur Q&A](https://kontur.ru/qa/28200), [buh.ru](https://buh.ru/news/s-2026-goda-ausn-smogut-primenyat-predprinimateli-bolee-80-regionov-.html), [spmag.ru list](https://spmag.ru/articles/v-kakih-regionah-dejstvuet-ausn-v-2026-godu-spisok-sp), **third-party**). For this service at 4–8 million ₽, compare 8% (320,000–640,000 ₽) with 6% minus contributions (240,000–480,000 ₽ of tax, of which contributions of 94,390–134,390 ₽ are part). The simplified system on income is cheaper at this scale. The automated system becomes interesting only between 20 and 60 million ₽, where its VAT exemption avoids the 5% VAT; its own end date of 2027 is a risk. The 54-FZ cash register duty still applies on it. Whether the operator's region has adopted it is **unconfirmed**.

## 4. Online cash registers (54-FZ) for an ИП on the simplified system

- **Required.** An ИП on the simplified system that accepts card or СБП payments online from individuals must issue an online-cash-register receipt for each payment and refund. The old exemption for an ИП without employees selling their own services ended in July 2021 ([Robokassa on 54-FZ](https://robokassa.com/payments/tarify/), [Kontur on YooKassa receipts](https://kontur.ru/qa/26435), **third-party**). Income under the professional income tax is exempt (see section 1).
- **Provider-included receipts** (the cheapest route, needing no cash register of one's own):
  - **Robokassa.** «Робочеки» are included in the tariff, "Отправка чеков 0 ₽" ([Robokassa tariffs](https://robokassa.com/payments/tarify/)).
  - **YooKassa.** The published card rate of 2.8% applies "при условии подключения сервиса «Чеки от ЮKassa»", and the tariff row reads "2,8% + 1% за чек", without VAT. The page does not define the base of the "1% за чек" fee or the rate without the receipt service ([YooKassa fees](https://yookassa.ru/fees/)). One review reads it as 1% of each receipt on top of 2.8% ([Quasa](https://quasa.io/ru/media/yukassa-ili-cloudpayments-podpiski-mogut-perevesit-stavku), **third-party**). The effective cost, possibly about 3.8% plus 22% VAT, is **unconfirmed**.
  - **CloudPayments.** "Облачные чеки" have no monthly fee, need no fiscal drive or fiscal data operator (ОФД), and are priced by the number of payments, without a published figure ([CloudPayments receipts](https://cloudpayments.ru/cloudkassir/check)).
  - **T-Bank** offers receipts with its internet acquiring; its price is **unconfirmed**.
- **Own cloud cash register**, if a provider does not include receipts:
  - rental is roughly 800–3,000 ₽ a month ([Yandex Pay blog, August 2026](https://pay.yandex.ru/blog/articles/onlajn-kassa-v-arendu), **third-party**);
  - ATOL Online shows tariffs from about 1,546–1,733 ₽ a month for new businesses and 2,425–2,650 ₽ a month on its basic tariff, with ОФД included in some tariffs ([ATOL Online](https://atol.online/), read through its price page);
  - Tochka rents one for 1,500 ₽ a month over a 36-month term, ОФД included ([Tochka](https://tochka.com/rko/oblachnaya-kassa/), **third-party** via search snippet).
- **Fiscal drive (ФН).** A 36-month drive costs about 18,600–20,400 ₽: Robokassa charges 19,425 ₽ from 2026-10-01 and ATOL's price list shows 18,605 ₽ ([Robokassa blog](https://robokassa.com/blog/articles/resurs-khraneniya-fd-ischerpan/), **third-party** aggregation of search snippets). That is about 6,500 ₽ a year.
- **Fiscal data operator (ОФД).** About 900–3,000 ₽ a year; Kontur.OFD charges 2,735 ₽ a year ([toolfox review](https://toolfox.ru/blog/ofd-2026-top-9-operatorov), **third-party**).
- **Totals.** With an own cloud cash register, about 1,500–3,000 ₽ a month, or 18,000–36,000 ₽ a year, all included. With provider receipts, 0 ₽ at Robokassa; per receipt and unpublished at YooKassa and CloudPayments.

## 7. Legal and accounting services (all prices third-party, Moscow-centric, "from" figures)

- **(a) Privacy policy, consent texts and user agreement or public offer.**
  - Template-based packages: a privacy policy from 3,500 ₽, consent forms from 2,500 ₽, and a package of notification, policy, protection regulation, consents and order for 11,500 ₽ ([vnesenie-v-reestr.ru](https://vnesenie-v-reestr.ru/roskomnadzor-services)).
  - A Moscow law firm drafts a user agreement for 15,000 ₽ ([dvitex.ru](https://www.dvitex.ru/docs/dogovory/podgotovka_polzovatelskogo_soglasheniya/)).
  - A marketing blog quotes "turnkey" 152-FZ website compliance from 75,000 ₽ ([sostav.ru](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/282100/62298)).
  - Expected range for a lawyer's review of an existing set of three documents: about 15,000–60,000 ₽. This is an **unconfirmed** estimate from the figures above.
- **(b) Written legal opinion on minors' (14–17) consent under 152-FZ.**
  - Public written consultations cost 15,000 ₽ without document study and 25,000 ₽ with it ([dvitex.ru written consultation](https://www.dvitex.ru/docs/konsultatsii/pismennaya_konsultatsiya_yurista_po_voprosu_klienta_s_izucheniem_dokumentov/)). An overview gives 5,000–30,000 ₽ for business questions in 2026 ([vitvet.com](https://vitvet.com/service/stoimost-uslug-yurista/)).
  - A formal opinion by a firm specialising in personal data is likely higher: about 30,000–100,000 ₽, **unconfirmed**, no price list found.
- **(c) Notification to Roskomnadzor (РКН).**
  - The notification is filed before processing starts, on paper or electronically through Roskomnadzor's portal, and no state fee for it was found. "Free" is **unconfirmed** in a primary source; 152-FZ sets none as far as the sources read show.
  - Outsourced filing costs 4,900 ₽, or 24,900 ₽ as a "comprehensive registration" with information-system classification and 12 months of support ([vnesenie-v-reestr.ru](https://vnesenie-v-reestr.ru/roskomnadzor-services)).
- **(d) Monthly outsourcing.**
  - Accounting for an ИП on the simplified system without employees: online services from about 458–1,565 ₽ a month (Moe Delo, Kontur), a personal accountant from about 2,240–6,100 ₽ a month, and Moe Delo's full outsourcing from 4,606 ₽ a month.   - Subscription legal service for business starts from 20,000 ₽ a month, and monthly consulting from 10,000 ₽ ([h-cons.ru](https://h-cons.ru/tseny)).

## 8. Penalties under 152-FZ, headline figures

Federal Law No. 420-FZ of 2024-11-30, in force from 2025-05-30, amended art. 13.11 of the Code of Administrative Offences. For commercial organizations and ИП ([ConsultantPlus news](https://www.consultant.ru/legalnews/28492)):

- failing to notify Roskomnadzor of processing: 100,000–300,000 ₽;
- failing to notify a leak: 1–3 million ₽;
- leaks: 3–5 million ₽ from 1,000 subjects, 5–10 million ₽ from 10,000, 10–15 million ₽ above 100,000, 10–15 million ₽ for special categories and 15–20 million ₽ for biometrics;
- a repeat leak: 1–3% of annual revenue, at least 20–25 million ₽ and at most 500 million ₽;
- general violations (part 1): ИП are fined 50,000–100,000 ₽, as officials are, and 300,000–500,000 ₽ for a repeat, as organizations are.

The 50% discount for early payment no longer applies to these fines.
