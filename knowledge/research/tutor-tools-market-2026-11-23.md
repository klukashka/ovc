# Tools Russian private tutors pay for, and market indicators: facts checked on 2026-11-23

Checked 2026-11-23. Prices, plans, limits, availability in Russia and survey figures are volatile; recheck them on each vendor's page before relying on them. This file is agent research for the business plan of a flat per-tutor price with free student accounts, and decides nothing. It adds to the [competitors research](competitors-2026-10-22.md), the [tutor CRM and mobile distribution research](tutor-crm-and-mobile-distribution-2026-10-25.md), the [tutoring market section](cost-levers-and-market-2026-10-25.md#tutoring-market) of the cost levers research and the [Weekify research](weekify-2026-11-23.md); it does not repeat what they hold except where a figure is needed for comparison or has changed.

## Method and conventions

- Every fact was checked on 2026-11-23 at the linked page, unless the text gives another date for the source itself. Official vendor pages, help centres, offers and store listings written by the developer are preferred. Sources that are not the vendor (catalogues, news, agencies, blogs) are marked **third-party**. Facts no reachable source confirmed are marked **unconfirmed**.
- Several vendor pages render prices only with JavaScript. Edvibe's price page was read in a browser after declining non-essential cookies. ProgressMe again answered with a Yandex SmartCaptcha, which was not bypassed. RuStore rate-limited automated reads after two listings.
- Foreign prices are converted at the Bank of Russia rate dated 2026-11-21: 1 USD = 82.1911 ₽ ([Bank of Russia daily rates](https://www.cbr.ru/currency_base/daily/)).
- "Per month" for an annual price is the annual sum divided by 12 (or by the vendor's own period), marked **derived**.
- What each product includes is summarised as four columns used in the closing table: **video** (a lesson room with calls inside the product), **homework** (assigned work the student answers in the product; "auto" when answers are checked automatically), **CRM** (students, schedule, booking), **finances** (payments, balances, debts).

## 1. Tutor CRMs

### Weekify (Викифай)

Reused from the [Weekify research](weekify-2026-11-23.md), checked the same day and not rechecked: 30 days free, then **350 ₽ per 30 days**, one plan with every feature, no card and no auto-renewal; no stated student limit. Includes students and parents' cabinet, weekly schedule template, balances with automatic debits, deposits and debts, income and expenses, 12 metrics, online booking, task tracker, notifications through VK, Telegram and MAX. No video room, no chat, homework only as a text field per lesson, no export.

### AlfaCRM

- **No plan for an individual tutor.** The [pricing page](https://alfacrm.pro/pricing) lists Старт 2,600 ₽ a month for up to 100 active clients (a client who attended a lesson in the last 30 days), Бизнес 3,900 ₽ for 200, and Персональный from 5,400 ₽ for 300 up to 12,500 ₽ above 1,000. Billing for 3, 6 or 12 months gives 5%, 6% or 20% off, so Старт billed yearly is about **2,080 ₽ a month** (derived).
- **Add-ons a tutor would want:** client portal, SMS or email, VK and push 400 ₽ each; internal chats 500 ₽; Telegram, MAX bot and Faster Payments System (СБП) acceptance 800 ₽ each; online schedule free. Bundles from 1,450 ₽.
- **Trial:** no length stated; the page says an unpaid test environment is deleted after 30 days.
- **Includes:** CRM and finances; no video room and no homework found (unchanged from the [competitors research](competitors-2026-10-22.md#alfacrm-the-crm-for-lessons-and-payments)).

### «Мой класс» (Moyklass)

- **Positioning:** a CRM for training centres whose Базовый plan the [pricing page](https://moyklass.com/prices/) addresses explicitly to "частный репетитор или небольшой учебный центр".
- **Price:** Базовая версия **from 743 ₽ a month**, PRO from 1,043 ₽; every plan has unlimited students, branches and staff. Which billing period gives 743 ₽ is not stated; the student app, sold on PRO only, costs 990 ₽ a month on 12 months, 1,090 ₽ on 6 and 1,190 ₽ on 1 or 3, so 743 ₽ is probably the 12-month rate (**unconfirmed**). The page mentions a free plan (staff app "на бесплатном — недоступно") and "Попробовать CRM бесплатно"; the free plan's limits and the trial length are **unconfirmed**. A "Black Friday" offer (pay 2 months get 3, and similar) runs until 27 November.
- **Includes on Базовый:** students, subscriptions (абонементы), leads, tasks, birthdays, mailings, contract templates, certificates for the tax service, reports, finances and cash desks, student cabinet, online payments, VK widgets. Internal messenger for staff only on Базовый. No video room or homework found.

### Hollihop

- [Pricing page](https://hollihop.ru/price) (prices include VAT): Микро **1,750 ₽ a month** for up to 100 active students (a student with a scheduled lesson in the next 7 days), with schedule, sales, communications and analytics; Класс 3,930 ₽ for 400 adds tasks; Класс Плюс 6,120 ₽ adds the finance module (income and expenses, debtors, cash desks and receipts, tax certificates, profit forecast); Профи individual. Yearly billing gives 16% off, so Микро is about **1,470 ₽ a month** yearly (derived). A mobile app and a client cabinet cost 1,000 ₽ a month extra below Профи.
- **Trial:** "Начать бесплатно"; length **unconfirmed**.
- For a tutor, debts and payment tracking start only at 6,120 ₽; a school CRM, not a tutor tool.

## 2. Finch

- **Identification:** Finch at [finch.study](https://www.finch.study/) is "платформа для независимых репетиторов" by ООО «Смайл Текнолоджис», Moscow, a company that says it has worked in educational technology since 2018 ([home page](https://www.finch.study/); [offer](https://www.finch.study/assets/policy/%D0%94%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BE%D1%80-%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%B5%D1%80%D1%82%D0%B0%20%D0%BD%D0%B0%20%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5%20%D1%83%D1%81%D0%BB%D1%83%D0%B3.pdf)). The Soware catalogue lists the same product and developer ([Soware](https://soware.ru/products/finch), **third-party**). Other products named Finch (a self-care app with a virtual pet, finance software) are unrelated to tutoring; finch.study is the only tutor-relevant match found, and that it is the product intended for comparison is **unconfirmed**.
- **Features** (home page): video calls on its "own video hosting" with screen sharing, claimed stable with up to 100 students; an interactive whiteboard with PDF and video insertion and graphics-tablet handwriting, saved to materials; individual and group lessons; schedule with day, week and month load and reminders; self-booking link that shows free slots; chat for materials, homework discussion and rescheduling; a student list with contacts, lesson price and progress; groups; payment marking (prepaid, partial, full) with automatic reminders to students; revenue and debt analytics. Students pay the tutor directly; the platform does not accept payments. Students use it free. Its help centre lists a "Домашние задания" widget and a PWA ([help centre](https://www.finch.study/finchhelp)); no auto-checked tests were found (**unconfirmed**). Soware lists web and Android, no iOS app, no API, no import or export (**third-party**).
- **Prices** (offer, appendix 1, and home page):
  - free: 7 days of full access after registration; afterwards free basic access with **at most 3 students**, without calls or whiteboard;
  - monthly modules, each 30 days: unlimited students **399 ₽**, whiteboard **99 ₽**, calls **99 ₽**; all three together **597 ₽ a month** (derived);
  - «Профи» package (calls, whiteboard, unlimited students): 1,399 ₽ for 90 days (≈466 ₽ a month), 2,599 ₽ for 180 days (≈433 ₽), **4,899 ₽ for 360 days (≈408 ₽ a month)**;
  - «Оптима» package (calls and whiteboard, still 3 students): 499 ₽, 899 ₽ and 1,699 ₽ for 90, 180 and 360 days (≈141–166 ₽ a month);
  - payment by card with automatic renewal until cancelled.
- **Scale claim:** "10 000+ занятий проведено" on the home page; no user count.
- **Relevance:** the closest Russian match to OVC's scope found so far at a price below 600 ₽: video room, whiteboard, chat, schedule, booking and payment tracking for one tutor. What it lacks against OVC, by its public pages: self-checking homework, recordings and an iOS app (**unconfirmed** from inside the product).

Only Yandex account holders create meetings; guests join by link.
- Free for every one-to-one or small-group lesson; no whiteboard, homework or CRM.

## 5. Market size indicators

- **Self-employed (НПД payers).** 17.263 million registered in early August 2026 against 15.43 million at the end of 2025, by the tax service's data platform `geochecki-vpd.nalog.gov.ru` as reported ([Sostav, 2026-08-21](https://www.sostav.ru/publication/chislo-samozanyatykh-v-rossii-prevysilo-17-2-mln-86292.html), **third-party**); 17.6 million by 1 October 2026 in another report ([AG Expert](https://www.advgazeta.ru/ag-expert/news/samozanyatost-v-tsifrakh/), **third-party**, page not read). The published category breakdown (repairs 1.19 million, transport 1.05 million, IT 0.67 million, beauty 0.56 million) does not list education or tutoring.
- **Number of tutors:** still **unconfirmed**; no official or survey figure was found. YouDo reported 20% more new tutor registrations in 2026 than a year earlier (search summary of an RBC Marketing item, **third-party**, not read in full). Avito reported 63% more responses to tutor job offers in the first quarter of 2026 year on year (**third-party**, via search summary).
- **Share teaching online:** 97% fully or partly, from the 2025 Avito and ProgressMe survey already recorded ([competitors research](competitors-2026-10-22.md#market-context-in-2026)); no 2026 figure found.
- **Lesson prices in 2026** (all **third-party**):
  - an average hour of 1,000–1,500 ₽ and 2,000–2,500 ₽ for exam preparation with experienced tutors, by an expert quoted by RIAMO ([RIAMO](https://riamo.ru/news/ekonomika/repetitory-bolshe-ne-po-500-rublej-skolko-teper-platjat-za-znanija/));
  - 1,500 ₽ average for an ОГЭ lesson, 14% more than a year earlier, by a Выберу.ру survey (search summary of [Mail.ru Finance](https://finance.mail.ru/article/raskryto-skolko-rossiyane-tratyat-na-podgotovku-k-oge-i-ege-69208286/));
  - about 1,500 ₽ a lesson in September 2026 ([Kommersant FM via Mail.ru Finance, 2026-09-07](https://finance.mail.ru/article/repetitorov-pozvali-ranshe-69226425/));
  - the 1,200–2,500 ₽ average and 10–20% yearly rise of May 2026 are in the [cost levers research](cost-levers-and-market-2026-10-25.md#tutoring-market);
  - parents of grade 7–8 pupils spend 4,300 ₽ a week on extra lessons and of grade 9 pupils 5,100 ₽, in a survey of 3,000 parents from 2 March to 14 April 2026 (search summary of an [RBC item](https://www.rbc.ru/life/news/69e87f759a79479d4c646e60), page refused automated reads).
- **Seasonality of exam preparation** (all **third-party**): Profi.ru reported 54% more demand for exam preparation in January–April 2026 than a year earlier ([Rossiyskaya Gazeta, 2026-05-13](https://rg.ru/2026/05/13/cennye-uroki.html)); Avito reported searches before the exams up 55% for history, 32% for geometry and 18% for mathematics in the first quarter of 2026 (search summary); in September 2026 group enrolment was reported to close earlier than usual ([Kommersant FM via Mail.ru Finance](https://finance.mail.ru/article/repetitorov-pozvali-ranshe-69226425/)). The usual pattern described in tutors' marketing material is a peak in September–October and a second rise in January–April before the exams in May–June, with a summer trough; no month-by-month platform data was found (**unconfirmed**). Tutors of adults are less exposed to it.
- **Marketplaces' charges to tutors:**
  - **Profi.ru:** two models on its [registration page](https://profi.ru/geo-kzn/registration/): pay per response, paid upfront, with "7–10 responses" usually needed for a first order; or a commission per won order with free responses, open to tutors after 10 paid responses. No amounts are published. A tutor's account of March 2026 gives 300–500 ₽ per response and about 100 responses before a first order; a commenter reports 40–200 ₽ ([T-J, 2026-03-13](https://t-j.ru/profi-ru-dlia-repetitorstva/), **third-party**, anecdotal). The page claims more than 500,000 orders a month.
  - **Repetit.ru:** the tutor terms page (`/for-teachers/`) returned 404; reviews mention a commission without a figure ([RBC Courses](https://www.rbc.ru/courses/school-reviews/repetit-ru), **third-party**). The commission is **unconfirmed**. Its site gives an average of about 1,500 ₽ an hour ([home page](https://repetit.ru/)).
  - **Repetitor.ru** (a different company) sells advertising campaigns to tutors through its tutor app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/repetitor-ru-%D0%B4%D0%BB%D1%8F-%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2/id1625876943)); prices **unconfirmed**.

## 6. Acquisition channels for tutors

- **Tutor communities in Telegram and VK:** no reliable member counts were found. The visible ones are classified-ad chat networks for tutors (for example a "ХОУ Репетиторы" network of city chats, described only in a promotional post, [TenChat](https://tenchat.ru/2624147), **third-party**), Telegram channels of individual tutors ([Skillbox Media list](https://skillbox.ru/media/education/podborka-avtorskikh-telegramkanalov-uchiteley-i-repetitorov/), **third-party**) and paid courses that teach tutors to run a channel. TGStat refused automated reads. Community sizes are **unconfirmed** and need a manual check in the apps.
- **VK Ads, education, 2026** (all **third-party**, agency and blog estimates, not VK data): online education is counted among competitive niches with CPM 200–500 ₽ and more, **CPC 50–150 ₽** and more, a community subscriber 200–300 ₽ and a **lead 1,500–5,000 ₽** ([Checkroi, updated 2026-09-18](https://checkroi.ru/blog/skolko-stoit-reklama-v-vk/)); CPC 10–40 ₽ in simple niches and 50–150 ₽ in competitive ones ([Workspace](https://workspace.ru/blog/skolko-stoit-reklama-v-vk-ads-v-2026-godu-byudzhet-na-test-zayavki-markirovku-i-dokumenty/)); one education case reports about 4,200 ₽ per lead from social networks ([KEDU, 2026-03-27, updated 2026-04-24](https://kedu.ru/press-center/articles/skolko-stoit-lid-v-obrazovanii-v-currentyear-godu/)).
- **Yandex Direct, 2026** (all **third-party**): no published education CPC was found; ordinary niches 30–120 ₽ a click and small regional niches 10–20 ₽ ([Checkroi, updated 2026-09-18](https://checkroi.ru/blog/skolko-stoit-reklama-v-yandekse/)); one education case gives about 3,100 ₽ per lead at 9% conversion ([KEDU](https://kedu.ru/press-center/articles/skolko-stoit-lid-v-obrazovanii-v-currentyear-godu/)); lead costs in online education rose 20–45% year on year by the same source.
- **Implication (estimate):** at 1,500–5,000 ₽ per lead from paid ads, a tutor plan of 350–600 ₽ a month repays a paid lead only after several months of retention; at 990–1,490 ₽, after about two to five months, before conversion from lead to paying tutor. The calculation ignores conversion rates, which no source gave for tutor software.
