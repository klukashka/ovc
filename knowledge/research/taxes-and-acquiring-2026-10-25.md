# Taxes and card acquiring for a small Russian subscription service: facts checked on 2026-10-25

Checked 2026-10-25. Tax rates, limits, contributions and acquiring fees are volatile and depend on the operator's legal form and contracts; confirm them with an accountant and the payment provider before relying on them. This file is agent research: it supplies evidence for the [profit estimate](../analysis/profit-estimate-2026-10-25.md) and decides nothing. It is not tax advice.

## Method and conventions

- Sources are tax-service, bank and accounting publications; none is the text of the Tax Code itself, so every figure is marked **third-party** unless stated otherwise.
- Facts no reachable source confirmed are marked **unconfirmed**.

## Professional income tax (НПД, the self-employed regime)

- Rates of 4% on income from individuals and 6% on income from legal entities and individual entrepreneurs, fixed until the end of 2028; an annual income limit of 2.4 million ₽, counted cumulatively, beyond which the right to the regime ends; a one-time deduction of 10,000 ₽ that, until used, lowers the 4% rate to 3% and the 6% rate to 4%; the regime runs as an experiment until 2028-12-31 ([Mail.ru Finance](https://finance.mail.ru/card/npd-961/), [Kontur](https://kontur.ru/articles/5337), **third-party**).
- A person on the professional income tax cannot employ workers under employment contracts; hiring means moving to another regime (general rule of the regime as described by the sources above, **third-party**).
- Whether selling subscriptions to one's own online service fits the regime was not found in a source; it is commonly read as the person's own service, which the regime allows (**unconfirmed**, ask the tax service or an accountant).

## Individual entrepreneur on the simplified system

- Fixed insurance contributions for 2026: 57,390 ₽ for a full year, due even with no income, plus 1% of annual income above 300,000 ₽, paid the following year ([buh.ru](https://buh.ru/news/v-2026-godu-uvelichilsya-razmer-fiksirovannykh-strakhovykh-vznosov-dlya-ip.html), [Regberry](https://www.regberry.ru/nalogooblozhenie/fiksirovannye-vznosy-ip-2026), **third-party**).
- The simplified system on income is commonly taxed at 6%, and an entrepreneur without employees can reduce that tax by the contributions paid (**unconfirmed** for 2026 in a source read here; general rule of the regime).
- The 2026 thresholds at which the simplified system starts charging VAT were not confirmed in a source read here (**unconfirmed**); they matter only far above the revenues in the profit estimate.

## Card and Faster Payments System acquiring

- YooKassa: fees "from 0.4% plus VAT" for successful payments, with cards, the Faster Payments System, SberPay, recurring payments and acquiring for the self-employed among its methods ([Timeweb community review](https://timeweb.com/ru/community/articles/platyozhnye-sistemy-dlya-obrazovaniya-v-2026-sravnenie-i-podvodnye-kamni), **third-party**); the lowest rates usually apply to the Faster Payments System, card rates are higher (**unconfirmed**).
- CloudPayments: subscriptions and the Faster Payments System are supported; its public calculator shows 2.6% as an example, and the actual rate is set by contract ([Timeweb community review](https://timeweb.com/ru/community/articles/platyozhnye-sistemy-dlya-obrazovaniya-v-2026-sravnenie-i-podvodnye-kamni), **third-party**).
- The earlier [cost levers and market research](cost-levers-and-market-2026-10-25.md#payments-for-a-tutor-service) records that YooKassa stopped supporting receipts of the self-employed on 2025-12-29, so a self-employed operator registers income in the tax service's app separately.

## Not verified

- Whether the subscription service fits the professional income tax regime.
- The 2026 VAT thresholds of the simplified system and the exact rule for reducing its tax by contributions.
