# Personal-data consent for students aged 14–17: practice of Russian services and the legal basis

Checked 2026-10-28. Every fact below was read on that date at the linked page, except those labelled `[unconfirmed]`, whose page could not be read or confirmed. Laws, regulator positions, service documents and fines are volatile; recheck them before relying on them. This file is agent research: it supplies evidence for [proposal 0003](../../docs/proposals/0003-students-aged-14-and-over.md) and the questions for a lawyer, and decides nothing. It is analysis, not legal advice. Quotations are short; everything else is paraphrased.

## Method and conventions

Each fact carries one label, and an agent's own inference is marked as such in the text:

- `[official]`: the text of a law (as published by ConsultantPlus) or a service's own document;
- `[commentary]`: a law firm, consultancy or professional publication;
- `[blog]`: a blog, news item or retelling of a speech;
- `[unconfirmed]`: no primary source confirmed it, or the page could not be read.

Services were read from their public documents and sign-up pages without creating an account. No CAPTCHA was bypassed.

## Services

### Umskul (umschool.net), an online exam-preparation school

- **Minimum age.** The customer under the offer is an adult legal representative or the student aged 14–17 acting "with the consent of the legal representative"; the customer warrants being at least 14 and, if 14–17, that the representative consented (offer, item 1.5). Nothing checks this warranty. `[official]` <https://umschool.net/documents/oferta/>
- **Legal basis of the main processing.** The privacy policy bases the learning platform's processing on the contract (the offer and the account agreement), item 5 of part 1 of article 6 of 152-FZ, not on consent; the contract is concluded at registration. Consent is used for enquiries, marketing and similar purposes. `[official]` <https://umschool.net/documents/privacy-policy/>
- **Interface.** The lead form says that pressing the button gives consent to processing; a marketing checkbox next to it is pre-ticked; there is no separate personal-data checkbox. `[official]` (observed on the page) <https://umschool.net/>
- **Messenger bot consent** is given by pressing "Start" in the bot, whose moment is recorded; the person thereby confirms their legal capacity. `[official]` <https://umschool.net/documents/agreement/>
- **Under 14.** The offer does not address it; the policy collects the name and contacts of the student's legal representative. `[official]` <https://umschool.net/documents/privacy-policy/>

### Webium (webium.ru), an online exam-preparation school

- **Minimum age.** The user warrants being at least 14 and, if 14–17, that the representative consented; the school may ask for proof that the course is paid from the minor's own income or for the representatives' written consent, and may refuse or suspend access without it. `[official]` <https://webium.ru/public-offer-agreement/>
- **Interface.** Under the sign-in button: pressing it accepts the offer and the privacy policy and gives consent to processing, where "consent" links to a separate consent document; a separate marketing checkbox is not pre-ticked. `[official]` (observed on the page) <https://webium.ru/>
- **Consent text** opens with the person confirming their legal capacity. `[official]` <https://webium.ru/personal-data-consent-for-auth-by-phone/>
- **Legal bases.** The policy lists "conclusion and performance of the contract" for the main purposes and consent for some others. `[official]` <https://webium.ru/privacy-policy/>

### 100points (100points.pro), an online exam-preparation school

- **Domain.** The domain first searched for, 100ballnik.com, belongs to an unrelated site; the school runs on 100points.pro. `[unconfirmed]` <https://100ballnik.com/>
- **Minimum age.** A user is a visitor who has reached 14; a younger user acts with a representative's consent (policy, item 2.10); nothing checks it. `[official]` <https://disk.yandex.ru/i/Hj00qj7PbG1CWA>
- **Consent.** Submitting a form counts as consent to the policy (item 6.1); the footer links "Policy" and "Consent" lead to the same file, so no separate consent document was found. `[official]` (observed on the page) <https://100points.pro/>
- **Offer.** The offer file could not be downloaded. `[unconfirmed]` <https://100points.pro/>

### Foxford (foxford.ru)

- **Not read.** The site answered 401 and 403 on two attempts, and a search did not find the agreement text. `[unconfirmed]` <https://foxford.ru/>

### Uchi.ru (uchi.ru), policy of 2025-11-19

- **Model.** A parent or a teacher creates the student's account; registering the child, the parent consents as legal representative; when a school or teacher creates it, the school warrants holding the parents' written consent; students are asked not to create profiles without their parents. There is no separate threshold at 14. `[official]` <https://uchi.ru/legal-content/privacy>

### Skysmart (legal.skysmart.ru)

- **Model.** The offer for children is concluded by a customer with full legal capacity, the legal representative, on behalf of the minor student, who is not a party. `[official]` <https://legal.skysmart.ru/doc/view/offerunfdkidsanorus>
- **Consent.** Registration of the customer and acceptance of the data-processing agreement count as consent for the customer's and the student's data; for data provided later, the first sign-in after providing it counts (item 11.2). `[official]` <https://legal.skysmart.ru/doc/view/offerunfdkidsanorus>
- **Site terms of 2022-03-28** use the model "by accessing or using the site you agree". `[official]` <https://legal.skysmart.ru/doc/view/terms_of_use_SKM_ANO_rus>
- **Privacy policy** is a scanned PDF without a text layer and could not be read. `[unconfirmed]` <https://legal.skyeng.ru/doc/view/privacypolicyanorus>

### Yandex ID and Yandex Uchebnik

- **General terms** (published 2026-10-10): a user from 14 may register and use the services alone; under 14, and where the law requires it, only with the parents' consent. `[official]` <https://yandex.ru/legal/rules/>
- **Children's accounts** are created by a parent in the family section of their own account. `[blog]` <https://mobile-review.com/all/news/yandeks-soobshhila-o-poyavlenii-speczialnyh-detskih-akkauntov/>
- **Uchebnik.** A student is a minor using the service with the knowledge and consent of a representative (item 1.2.5); the parent accepts the terms (item 1.2.7); the terms are accepted by starting to use the service (item 1.3). `[official]` <https://yandex.ru/legal/uchebnik_termsofuse/>

### VK (vk.com)

- **Terms not read.** The terms page rendered empty on two attempts. `[unconfirmed]` <https://vk.com/terms>
- **Minimum age.** Press reports say the VK terms admit registration from 14 without age verification. `[blog]` <https://www.securitylab.ru/news/485862.php>

### Sferum (sferum.ru)

- **Model.** Teachers, students, administrators and a parent or legal representative role; users join through their school. `[official]` <https://www.sferum.ru/terms_of_use>
- **Consent.** "By registering and using the system" the user agrees to the policy (item 1.6) and confirms holding the necessary consents (item 2.1); no minimum age was found in those items. `[official]` <https://www.sferum.ru/privacy_policy>

### Profi.ru

- **Minors.** Accepting the terms, the user confirms sufficient legal capacity; otherwise a parent or legal representative reads and accepts them on the user's behalf; no age is named and nothing is checked. `[official]` <https://profi.ru/documents/terms-of-use/>
- **Specialists** (tutors among them) must be over 18 and fully capable. `[official]` <https://profi.ru/documents/oferta/>

**What no service does:** none of the services read verifies a parent's consent for a free self-registration at 14–17 (no link to a parent's email, parent account, paper form or public-services login). Two models exist: a teenager from 14 registers alone (Umskul and Webium with a self-warranty of a parent's consent; 100points, Yandex and, by press reports, VK without one), or the parent is the party and the author of consent (Uchi.ru, Skysmart, Yandex Uchebnik).

## Law

### Consent kept separate: part 1 of article 9 of 152-FZ as amended by 156-FZ of 2025-06-24

- **Wording.** Consent is given freely, by one's own will and in one's own interest; it must be specific, informed, conscious and unambiguous, in any form that allows its receipt to be confirmed; from 2025-09-01 it is set out separately from other information and documents the person confirms or signs; when a representative gives it, the operator checks their authority. `[official]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/6c94959bc017ac80140621762d2ac59f6006b08c/>
- **Representatives.** Part 6 of article 9 has the legal representative consent for a person without legal capacity; 152-FZ has no separate rule for minors aged 14–17. `[official]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/6c94959bc017ac80140621762d2ac59f6006b08c/>
- **A checkbox with a link** is acceptable when the text next to it says plainly that it is consent to personal-data processing and it is not combined with accepting the offer. `[commentary]` <https://ppt.ru/amp/question/personal-data/oformlenie-soglasiya-na-obrabotku-personalnykh-dannykh-na-sayte>
- **Separate, unticked checkboxes** for personal data and for marketing with an active link; a pre-ticked box is not consent, which the firm attributes to the regulator without citing a document. `[commentary]` <https://h-cons.ru/stati/kak-pravilno-oformit-chekboks-soglasiya-na-obrabotku-pdn-sajte>
- **A separate page or pop-up** with the consent text; combined checkboxes are to be removed; the authors' view that consents collected under the old rules must be collected again is not confirmed by an official source. `[commentary]` <https://ppt.ru/columns/rosco/soglasie-na-obrabotku-personalnykh-dannykh-kak-izbezhat-shtrafa>
- **Separate document** from 2025-09-01. `[commentary]` <https://www.garant.ru/article/1862510/>
- **The regulator's own explanation** of whether a checkbox with a link, or a "by pressing…" button, suffices was not found. `[unconfirmed]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/6c94959bc017ac80140621762d2ac59f6006b08c/>
- **"By continuing to use the site you agree"** is supported as personal-data consent by no source read; the linked commentary requires an explicit act. Agent's inference from the text of article 9: unambiguous consent in a form that confirms its receipt sits poorly with silence. `[commentary]` <https://ppt.ru/amp/question/personal-data/oformlenie-soglasiya-na-obrabotku-personalnykh-dannykh-na-sayte>

### When no consent is needed: item 5 of part 1 of article 6 of 152-FZ

- **Contract.** Processing needed to perform a contract to which the person is a party, or to conclude one at their request, needs no consent; the exam-preparation schools above rely on it for their main learning processing. `[official]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/>
- **For OVC**, by the agent's inference from item 5 of part 1 of article 6, this basis works only if the teenager lawfully concluded the contract (the user agreement) themselves, the question of the next sections. `[unconfirmed]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/>

### Who consents for a person aged 14–17

- **"Alone from 14".** At the regulator's conference in November 2018 a representative of the regulator said orally that capacity regarding personal data starts at 14 and that consent signed by a child of 14 or older would not be challenged; no written document of the regulator says so. `[blog]` <https://www.securitylab.ru/blog/personal/emeliyannikov/345173.php>
- **Argument for 14.** A privacy author argues from the passport issued at 14, laws that require the 14–18-year-old's own consent, and article 28 of the Civil Code. `[blog]` <https://www.securitylab.ru/blog/personal/emeliyannikov/296078.php>
- **"Alone, with a parent's approval".** Citing article 26 of the Civil Code and article 64 of the Family Code, a commentator advises that a 14–17-year-old signs consent themselves with a parent's approval until 18. `[commentary]` <https://ppt.ru/art/personal-data/soglasie-na-obrabotku-personalnykh-dannykh-nesovershennoletnego>
- **Under 14.** Article 28 of the Civil Code (parents make transactions for children under 14) is cited as the reason a representative consents for them. `[commentary]` <https://spmag.ru/articles/soglasie-na-obrabotku-personalnyh-dannyh-rebenka>
- **Educational organisations** are often advised to take two consents, the minor's and the representative's. `[unconfirmed]` <https://ppt.ru/art/personal-data/soglasie-na-obrabotku-personalnykh-dannykh-nesovershennoletnego>

### A free service as a small household transaction

- **Article 26 of the Civil Code.** People aged 14–18 may alone make small household transactions and those of item 2 of article 28, including receiving a benefit free of charge; other transactions need the representatives' written consent. `[official]` <https://www.consultant.ru/law/podborki/gk_sdelki_nesovershennoletnih/>
- **Accepting a free online service's user agreement** is not expressly classed as such a transaction by any source found; one privacy author thinks item 2 of article 28 may cover it. `[unconfirmed]` <https://www.securitylab.ru/blog/personal/emeliyannikov/296078.php>

### Fines: article 13.11 of the Code of Administrative Offences

- **Part 2** (processing without written consent where it is required, or consent lacking required details): citizens 10,000–15,000 ₽; officials 100,000–300,000 ₽; legal entities 300,000–700,000 ₽. `[official]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_34661/1f421640c6775ff67079ebde06a7d2f6d17b96db/>
- **Part 2.1** (repeat): citizens 15,000–30,000 ₽; officials 300,000–500,000 ₽; individual entrepreneurs 500,000–1,000,000 ₽; legal entities 1,000,000–1,500,000 ₽. `[official]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_34661/1f421640c6775ff67079ebde06a7d2f6d17b96db/>
- **Part 1** (processing without a lawful basis): legal entities 150,000–300,000 ₽, 300,000–500,000 ₽ on repeat. `[official]` <https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_34661/1f421640c6775ff67079ebde06a7d2f6d17b96db/>
- **Which part applies** to a defective electronic consent on a website, since part 2 speaks of written consent, is a question for a lawyer; several web articles quote outdated amounts. `[unconfirmed]` <https://its.1c.ru/db/content/newscomm/src/487122.htm>

## Conclusions

Analysis, not legal advice.

- **Common practice at 14–17:** self-registration from 14, at two of the schools with a self-warranty that a parent consented; nobody verifies the parent for a free sign-up; the parent becomes the party when the parent pays or the child is under 14.
- **"Automatic" consent** that users remember is mostly the contract basis: the main processing follows from the accepted user agreement, and separate consent covers only what goes beyond it (marketing, recordings, transfers).
- **The lightest interface the sources accept:** the main features under the user agreement accepted at sign-up; for anything beyond it, a separate unticked checkbox that says it is consent to personal-data processing and links to a separate consent document; marketing on its own unticked checkbox; the consent's time and text version recorded. A "by pressing…" button without a checkbox is common but no source called it sufficient after 2025-09-01.

## Questions for a lawyer

Analysis, not legal advice: these questions frame the assessment that the roadmap requires before the pilot.

1. May a person aged 14–17 accept OVC's free user agreement alone, as a transaction under item 2 of article 26 or item 2 of article 28 of the Civil Code, so that the main processing of their data rests on item 5 of part 1 of article 6 of 152-FZ without consent?
2. If not, is the teenager's own consent enough, or is a parent's consent or approval needed, and is a self-warranty that a parent agreed sufficient?
3. Who is the party to the agreement and who is the operator of a student's personal data when the tutor (an individual entrepreneur or self-employed) is paid for lessons and the platform is free for the student: the platform, the tutor, or both under a processing instruction?
4. Does a "by pressing…" button linking to a separate consent document satisfy part 1 of article 9 as amended by 156-FZ, or is a separate unticked checkbox needed, and what must be kept as proof of consent?
5. Do video lesson recordings, their storage and showing a student's image to other participants need separate consent, and is a recorded image biometric data under article 11 when it is not used for identification?
6. Which part of article 13.11 applies to a defective website consent, and how is an operator who is an individual entrepreneur or self-employed liable?
7. Is age verification required, and what must happen when a user turns out to be under 14: deletion, blocking or asking for a parent's consent?
8. Must consents obtained before 2025-09-01 be collected again (relevant only when existing students are imported)?
9. How must OVC handle a legal representative's request to withdraw consent for, or delete, a minor's data: how the representative's authority is checked, the deadline, and whether blocking without deletion is ever lawful?
