Phone mockups, 360 px

Mockups of slice 10 change G0 for the owner's review: the phone frame below 768 px and one or more screens of each page group G1 to G5. The specifications in navigation.md, pages.md, tokens.md and states.md are normative; where a mockup differs from them, the specifications win. All data is invented. Each frame is 360 × 740 CSS px; its outline is only for the gallery. The interface copy inside the frames is Russian, as in the clickable prototype.

G1 · Frame

The bottom bar of five items with 20 px icons, the top bar with the logo and the Notifications button, the More sheet with the account, sections, Settings and Sign out, the unconfirmed student's reduced frame, and the notifications panel.

G2 · Tutor's pages

Schedule (an agenda by day), the Lesson page joining only in a new tab, Students and the Student card.

G3 · Student's and public pages

The student's Home and the invitation page (a page with a token in its link).

G4 · Homework

Library, the student's assignment page and the review screen.

G5 · Messages

The list and the conversation are separate screens; the conversation screen hides the bottom bar and the top bar.
