# Pages, element by element

Every page of the web application. Addresses and frames are in [navigation](navigation.md); state words and colours are in [states](states.md); values are in [tokens](tokens.md). Each page lists the requirements it covers. Every page shows the shared loading, empty, error and offline behavior described at the end.

Recordings (FR-5.4 to FR-5.6) and analytics (FR-11.2 to FR-11.4) come [after the MVP](../../../docs/roadmap.md#after-the-mvp) by the owner's decision of 2026-10-22. Until then the lesson page has no Recordings block or empty state, the student card has no Progress tab, the issue page has no Analytics tab, and Settings shows neither recording retention nor recording storage; the parts below that describe them are the later design.

## Public pages

The legal documents, the consent link, the acceptance line and the invitation page's policy link below follow [ADR-0024](../../../docs/decisions/0024-legal-documents-and-student-data.md), as specified by change L0 of the [interface rework plan](../../plans/ui-rework.md) on 2026-11-23 (pending owner review): the documents, the consent link and the acceptance line are pending implementation by L1, and the invitation page's policy link by L2. The interface carries no legal wording beyond the consent checkbox's label and the acceptance line; explanations, warranties and disclosures live in the documents.

### Sign up

FR-1.1, FR-1.2.

- `/signup` offers two large choices, each a bordered panel: "I teach" and "I study".
- **Form:**

  - one line under the submit button, the acceptance line: "By creating an account, you accept the user agreement" ("Создавая аккаунт, вы принимаете пользовательское соглашение"), the words "user agreement" linking `/terms`; pressing the button accepts the agreement, the checkbox never does (pending implementation by L1);
  - the student form (`/signup/student`) also has a required tutor code field, first in the form, with the hint "Got a link from your tutor? Open it instead"; an invalid code (`tutor_code_invalid`) shows "This code is no longer valid — ask your tutor for a new one" under the field, with the form's alert "Check the highlighted fields", and creates nothing; a valid code shows "Check your email" for a new and a taken email alike.
- **Submitting** shows "Check your email" with the address and a resend button, disabled for 60 seconds.
- **After confirmation** in the same browser, a student who signed up with a code sees "Request sent. Waiting for the tutor."
- **Phone** (slice 10 change G0, page group G3, pending owner review): "I teach" and "I study" stack as full-width panels; the form is one column with the tutor code first on `/signup/student`, the consent checkbox on a 44 px target and the submit button at the full width; "Check your email" puts its resend button at the full width.

Its address `/invite/{token}` is replaced with `/invite`; opened as `/invite` alone (for example after a reload), it shows "Open the link from your tutor again".
- **Phone** (slice 10 change G0, page group G3, pending owner review): the tutor's avatar, name and subjects stack above the invitation sentence; "Create account" and "Sign in to accept" (or "Accept" and "Use another account") stack at the full width, each a target of at least 44 px: "Create account", "Sign in to accept" and "Accept" as buttons, and "Use another account" as a full-width text button with the look it has on computers, because the tutor-explanation panel shares that sign-out control (owner's decision of 2026-11-22, recorded by slice 10 change A1, pending owner review); and the forms open in place in one column. ### Legal documents

FR-1.1. The privacy policy, the consent text and the user agreement ([ADR-0024](../../../docs/decisions/0024-legal-documents-and-student-data.md), change L0 of the [interface rework plan](../../plans/ui-rework.md), pending owner review; pending implementation by L1):

- **Addresses:** `/privacy`, `/consent` and `/terms` ([public addresses](navigation.md#public-addresses)), reachable signed in or out in the public frame, with the document's title as the page heading.
- **Texts** live in the repository as `frontend/src/legal/{ru,en}/{privacy,consent,terms}.md` (owner's decision of 2026-11-23), part of the frontend build like the interface catalogues. The Russian text is the binding version and the English one its translation, which says so in its own text. Each file records its version, its date and its review status (`draft` until a lawyer has reviewed it, then `reviewed`); the page shows the version and date under the heading. No text is published before the lawyer's review at the first deployment, as [ADR-0024](../../../docs/decisions/0024-legal-documents-and-student-data.md) and the [roadmap](../../../docs/roadmap.md#before-the-pilot) require, and a check fails when a deployment configuration exists while any text is `draft`.
- **Rendering:** each text is written in the [restricted Markdown](../../specs/homework.md#restricted-markdown) subset and rendered by its one shared renderer, never as raw HTML, in the interface language; a section title is a bold paragraph and a table is written as a list, because the renderer is not widened for these pages. The pages load nothing from a third party and keep the content security policy and `frame-ancestors 'none'` unchanged.
- **The version accepted:** the consent's version and time are recorded at sign-up ([registration](../../specs/accounts.md#registration-and-email-verification)); the user agreement accepted is the version in force at the account's creation, as the file's history shows.
- **Phone:** one column in the public frame on phones, the text at `text-body-phone`.

### Confirmation, password reset, unsubscribe

FR-1.1, FR-1.5, FR-13.3. Single-purpose pages with one result message and one next step. The confirmation page refuses an unknown, expired, used, superseded or malformed link at once, before any password is asked for; the reset page learns that the link is invalid only when the new password is sent. The confirmation page, opened in a browser not signed in to that account, first asks for a new password (with the strength hint) and then confirms; it then shows the outcome with a "Continue" link to the home page: "You are now studying with your tutor" (`linked`), "Request sent. Waiting for the tutor." (`request_sent`), "This code is no longer valid — enter a new one" (`code_invalid`); with no outcome it goes straight to the home page, and for `choice_required` it continues to the [reclaim page](#keep-or-drop-earlier-links). The reset page continues to sign-in, after which an account with a pending choice lands on the reclaim page; when the reset answers `choice_required` it adds "After you sign in, choose which earlier links and requests to keep". Unsubscribe names the email group and asks the user to confirm with a button (nothing changes when the page merely loads), then offers a link to all email settings. Token pages remove the token from the address bar after reading it.

- **Unsubscribe** (`/unsubscribe?token=`, slice 4) works without signing in and never signs anyone in. The token handling below is the contract; the page's messages, button and focus were built in slice 4 as proposed here and await the owner's confirmation:
  - it reads the token once on its first render, replaces the address with `/unsubscribe` before any request, and keeps the token only in component state, never in the query cache, storage, history state, `next` or any other address; opened as `/unsubscribe` alone it shows the invalid-link message;
  - it loads `GET /unsubscribe/preview`, showing "Checking the link" meanwhile, and then the group's name with the button "Turn off these emails"; every `token_invalid`, on the preview as on the confirmation, shows one message, "This link is no longer valid", which takes focus, with the link "All email settings" to Settings → Notifications (which asks to sign in when signed out); `rate_limited` shows the throttling message with the minutes to wait; any other failed preview shows the shared load failure with Retry;
  - confirming sends `POST /unsubscribe` with no body; the button stays busy until the answer, and the result "These emails are turned off" replaces the button and takes focus, with the link "All email settings" to Settings → Notifications (which asks to sign in when signed out); a refused confirmation other than `token_invalid` shows its error, which takes focus, and keeps the button;
  - the page's texts are in [states](states.md#notifications).
- **Phone** (slice 10 change G0, page group G3, pending owner review): one column with the result message and its one next step as a full-width button or link; the new-password form of the confirmation page and the reset page is one column with the strength hint under the field. The token handling above is unchanged (T-27).

### Schedule

## Shared behavior

- **Loading:**
  - skeletons in the shape of the content appear after 300 ms; nothing appears before;
  - buttons that start an action show a spinner and stay unavailable until it completes: they carry `aria-disabled="true"` and `aria-busy="true"` and ignore activation, but never the `disabled` attribute, which moves focus to the page body in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit; so a busy button keeps focus, and a button waiting for a cooldown (the confirmation resend) is unavailable the same way;
  - every sign-out control (the account menu's Sign out, from slice 10 change G1 the More sheet's Sign out on phones, the public header's Sign out and the invitation page's sign-out in place) is busy in this way while its sign-out runs, which can take up to 15 seconds while the tab's reads in flight are answered ([navigation](navigation.md)): it reads "Signing out…" and ignores further presses until the sign-out ends or fails (slice 8 review follow-up, pending owner review);
  - where several controls share one action (the Accept, Decline and menu buttons of every join-request row, the Remove buttons of the group members, the Unblock buttons of the blocked students, Send and "Create a new link" of an invitation link), only the control that started it carries `aria-busy="true"`; the others carry only `aria-disabled="true"` until it completes; a decision chosen from a row's menu marks that row's menu button busy.
- **Empty states:** a heading, one sentence and one primary action (an optional line illustration as in [tokens](tokens.md#illustration-and-iconography)):

  | Page | Message | Action |
  |---|---|---|
  | Messages | "No conversations yet" | Open a student or group |
  | Review queue | "Nothing to check" | — |
  | Issued work | "Nothing issued yet" | Open the library |
  | Groups | "No groups yet" | Create a group |
  | Student homework tabs | "Nothing to do" / "Nothing submitted" / "Nothing checked yet" / "Nothing closed" | — |
  | Recordings | "No recordings for this lesson" | — |

- **Errors:**
  - a failed page load shows "Something went wrong" with Retry and the request identifier in small text;
  - a failed background or live refetch of a student card or a group page whose data has loaded keeps the page, its data and the unsaved typing of its fields, and shows above them the notice "The latest data could not be loaded. The page shows what was loaded before." with Retry and the request identifier, announced by a polite live region without taking focus; this holds for every refusal of the refetch, `email_not_verified` included, except a missing card or group (`not_found`), which is shown as its page section defines, and a refusal that ends the session, which clears the loaded data; after Retry, once the notice is gone, focus moves once to the page heading when it was lost;
  - a forbidden or missing resource shows Not found;
  - failed actions show an error toast with Retry, reachable by keyboard;
  - uploads show the refusal inline: "File is larger than {limit}", "WebP pictures can be at most 8 MB", "This file type is not allowed here", "Storage is full";
  - a page whose subject a refetch finds gone shows its page's notice in place of its content ("This student is no longer on your list.", "This chat is no longer available.", from slice 9 "This assignment is no longer available."), which takes focus when focus was lost or a dialog was open;
  - a stale edit (a template, a review draft) shows "This changed elsewhere" with "Reload" and keeps the user's unsaved text visible;
  - the edit forms of a student card (name, email, notes) and a group (name, colour) stay mounted while they are shown, so focus stays where the page section puts it (from design slice step D5, pending owner review, the name, email, colour and group name fields live in dialogs that mount their fields with the current stored values when they open, and the rule below applies while a dialog is open); when a field's stored value changes, the field shows it at once only while the user has not changed the field since it last showed a stored value, so a background refetch never overwrites typing; after a reload because the card or group changed meanwhile (a refusal that shows the changed-meanwhile notice, or an edit refused as stale), every field whose stored value changed since it last showed one shows the current value, and the other fields keep the unsaved typing;
  - autosaved notes (design slice step D5, pending owner review) follow the same rule: while the notes have changes not yet saved, being saved or refused, a refetch never writes a stored value into the field; once a save has answered and nothing was typed after it, the field again shows a stored value only while it has not changed since it last showed one; a save that answers after newer typing never overwrites that typing, which the next save sends.
- **Offline** (slice 6, with chat, whose composer first needs it; until then pages keep working through REST while the realtime client reconnects): a banner "No connection — reconnecting…" in the signed-in frame, on every signed-in page, above the page's content and below the narrow top bar, outside the page itself, so it never takes focus and the page's heading stays first; it appears once the realtime client has had no `ready` socket for 5 seconds on the injected scheduler, so it does not flash on every reconnect, and a polite live region announces it once when it appears and once when the connection returns. Implemented once in the shared components, so pages do not repeat it.
  - Only the chat writes are disabled while it shows (the owner's decision of 2026-10-29, pending owner review): Send, Edit, Delete and attaching in Messages carry `aria-disabled="true"` with the reason "No connection" in their tooltip and accessible description, as the shared busy rule does, and ignore activation, never the `disabled` attribute, so focus stays; reads, navigation and typing continue, and the composer keeps its text.
  - Every other action stays enabled and works through REST, which does not depend on the WebSocket; assignment answers are saved through REST as usual.
  - The realtime client reconnects with backoff and refetches visible data on reconnect (slice 4).
- **Offline lifecycle:** the grace begins with the signed-in account, including its first-ready wait; repeated failed reconnects do not restart it. A new account epoch resets state and ignores old callbacks. Sign-out clears state without announcing recovery; pagehide cancels the timer and a BFCache restore resumes connection tracking. Offline guarding is opt-in for chat writes and applies to submit handlers as well as buttons, preserving independent busy/unavailable states after recovery.
- **Live data:** frames from the realtime channel trigger a refetch of the affected item and never replace it directly ([live refetch mapping](../../architecture/detailed.md#live-refetch-mapping)). Lists keep the scroll position.
  - **Show more during a refetch** (fix of the Show more refetch cancellation, pending owner review): "Show more", and the Retry of a failed next page, never cancels a refetch of the loaded pages that is in flight, such as the refetch after the realtime channel's `ready`; it waits for that refetch to answer and then, when the refetch succeeded and still has a next page, loads it from the refreshed cursor and moves focus to the first new row as usual; otherwise it loads nothing, and the failed refetch notice or the rule for a page that adds no visible row applies. Cancelling it would keep the pages as they were before the refetch and lose a change the refetch exists to catch.
  - **Focus on live refetch:** a refetch that keeps the focused element leaves focus where it is. When a live refetch removes the focused row of a list, focus moves to the row that took its place, else to the previous row, else to the list's heading, or to the page's heading for a list without one or a list that disappears with its last row. When it removes the subject of an open dialog, the dialog closes and the page's notice that says so takes focus: "This student is no longer on your list.", "This request was already answered." and similar, each defined by its page; for a dialog without such a notice, the page's changed-meanwhile notice is used. Each of these moves has a `toBeFocused` test.
  - **Pages with live refetch in slice 4:** Students (its tabs and pending requests), the student card, groups and the group page, Today's join-request panel, My tutors and student Home; from slice 5 also the Schedule and the lesson page, through `entity.changed` of kind `lesson`; from slice 6 Messages and the sidebar's unread count, through the message frames, `read.updated` and `entity.changed` of kind `channel`; from slice 9 the issued list, the issue page, the review queue and the review screen, the Homework counts, Today's Needs checking and Upcoming deadlines, the student card's Homework tab and Overview, the group page's issued work, the lesson page's assignments, the student's Homework lists, the assignment page and Home's To do and Recently checked, through `entity.changed` of kinds `issue` and `submission` ([live refetch mapping](../../architecture/detailed.md#live-refetch-mapping)).
- **Time:**
  - every time is shown in the viewer's timezone;
  - the lesson page adds the other party's time when it differs, using the `time.other_zone` string from [states](states.md#time-and-scores) with the city of the IANA timezone taken from a bundled, localised table loaded with the lesson page's code, and `time.other_offset` with the `UTC±hh:mm` offset for a zone the table does not list;
  - deadlines show both an absolute time and a relative hint ("in 2 days").
- **Desktop layout** (design slice step L, decision 7 of the [interface decisions](../../plans/ui-ux-decisions-2026-11-06.md#decisions), pending owner review): from 1024 px every signed-in page follows this standard, and pages reach it step by step through the design slice:

- **Responsive:**

  - every page, for both roles, is designed for a 1280 px computer screen first and degrades to a single column ([ADR-0010](../../../docs/decisions/0010-quiet-visual-language.md));
  - below 768 px every page follows its **Phone** rule above inside the [phone frame](navigation.md#frames) (slice 10 change G0, pending owner review; built by page group in changes G1 to G5):

    - tab lists and filter rows wrap rather than scroll sideways, and every target is at least 44 × 44 px;
    - outline icons are 20 px.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:**
  - every action is reachable by keyboard in visual order, with a visible focus ring;
  - dialogs trap focus and restore it to the opener on close, unless the page section names another target (a notice) or the action changes the route, as for every route change; a dialog opens with focus on the element its page section names, a destructive confirmation without fields on its safe choice (Cancel), one that asks for a password on that field, and otherwise on its first field;
  - a page's loading, error and refetching states render inside the page, so they never take focus away; after Retry, once the error is gone, focus moves once to the page heading when focus was lost; when a live refetch removes a load failure while focus is inside it (on its Retry), focus moves to the page heading, or, for a load failure inside a panel, to that panel's heading (slice 10 change A1, pending owner review);
  - from slice 4 each route's page code loads on demand: while it loads, the route's page shows its heading with the delayed loading state, and when the loaded page replaces it, focus that was on the loading page's heading moves to the loaded page's heading; when the code cannot be loaded, the page shows its heading and the alert "This page could not be loaded. Check your connection and reload the page." with the button "Reload the page", which reloads the document, because a browser keeps a failed module load for the life of the document;
  - "Show more" moves focus to the first new row; when the loaded page adds no visible row (its rows are all left out, as accepted requests are under Sent requests and students who are not active or already members are in the member choice) and "Show more" disappears with focus on it, focus moves to the last row shown, or, when no row is shown, to the page heading (in the member choice, to its search field);
  - a notice that reports a refusal takes focus when it appears, and again each time it reports a new refusal; a notice that reports a result takes focus when it replaces or follows the control that triggered it and focus was lost or was on that control; otherwise a live region announces it;
  - live regions announce new messages, saving status and toasts;
  - icons that act as buttons have accessible names;
  - charts have a table alternative;
  - every page has a "Skip to content" link, and route changes move focus to the page heading;
