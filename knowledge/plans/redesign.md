# Visual redesign implementation plan

## 1. Problem, outcome and scope

The approved visual target is ahead of OVC's implemented interface and its normative specifications. This plan makes the differences explicit, separates appearance from new product behavior, and proposes small changes that retain existing authorization, focus and resource-lifecycle contracts.

The inspected baseline is `main` at `4de0ac48eefd7602e6dfe699966c0ea85c028292`. There is no remote, CI service or issue tracker. The 20 visual references live in `knowledge/design/web/prototype/redesign/` and remain non-normative.

**Approval scope:** this draft seeks approval as an umbrella sequencing plan only; on approval it authorizes no implementation branch. Each R-branch needs its own owner-approved task plan, citing its `knowledge/research/ui-reference-<screen>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` study under the AGENTS.md UI work rule and mapping the scenarios below to named executable tests.

**Outcome proposed:** both roles receive the approved warm-paper themes, a flexible frame, consistent actions, the revised messenger, home pages and teaching screens. A local camera/microphone preview precedes the existing authorized room join. A public welcome page describes implemented capabilities. BigBlueButton's own room remains a separate research question, not an OVC whiteboard implementation.

**In scope:** the 20 visual references; both themes; desktop layouts; Messages and lobby phone layouts; accessible panel resizing; required contract amendments, reference studies, focused tests and rendered reviews in later implementation branches. Existing pages not drawn separately inherit shared tokens and action rules and must be reviewed when those changes affect them.

**Out of scope:** phone redesign of every other page, retained for `PHONE`; a new conferencing client; recordings before their approved roadmap stage; payment/subscription implementation; FIN, BOOK and TASK functionality; imported personal data; new analytics or external font/design services. Existing phone usability, 320 px reflow, 200% text resizing and 44 px coarse-pointer targets remain mandatory, including on pages whose dedicated phone design is deferred.

This follows the structure of [the interface rework plan](ui-rework.md) and the obligations of [the reference-study and design-review plan](ui-reference-and-design-review.md). Specifications and decision records stay normative until approved implementation changes amend them. A mockup is evidence of visual intent, not authority to relax a server rule or publish a commercial promise.

## 9. Risks and sequencing constraints

- **Bundle budget:** recorded entry sizes are about 182.6–183.3 KB of 184 KB, leaving about 0.7–1.4 KB, per [the slice 10.3 review](../reviews/slice-10-3-slice-review.md) and [the U2 bundle after-fix record](../reviews/u2-check-bundle-after-fix-2026-11-23.md). Re-measure at task-plan time. R1a/R1b estimate their added size and use a lazy separator/width state machine or code proven to fit measured headroom; move more texts into lazy feature sections. If that is insufficient, ask the owner before proceeding; never silently raise the budget. Shared frame additions can inflate the entry. Keep screen texts in lazy feature/language sections as ui-rework D12 requires; no eager welcome copy, emoji package or media code. `check:bundle` stays mandatory; do not raise its limits.
- **Time budget:** NFR-6.2’s full-run budget remains 600 s, including e2e. Recent gate wall times are about 650–730 s. The temporary allowance in [known limitations](../reviews/slice-10-known-limitations.md) and ADR-0018 changes only the threshold for asking the owner before merging a passed gate: 720 s or more requires that decision; a passed gate from 600 s to less than 720 s still breaches the budget but temporarily needs no per-change question. The allowance ends at the earliest of the gate speed-up merge, FIN’s first feature-plan approval or a reordering of the sub-slices after UI, as ADR-0018 specifies. After it ends, every passed gate of 600 s or more goes to the owner before merging. Refresh the allowance and evidence at task-plan time; every branch fills in an e2e cost estimate and projected gate time. Changing e2e files invokes the whole-suite row. Consolidate fixtures and avoid duplicated journeys; never drop negative or focus coverage, raise budgets or run measurements concurrently to meet a deadline.
- **Work in flight:** gate speed-up branches and the FIN plan are not authority to alter current checks/order. Refresh their actual merged state before each implementation plan; coordinate shared frame, Students, Schedule and Settings changes, and record responsibility rather than merging another worktree's work.
- **Notification dependency:** current baseline still imports `NotificationsBell` and has `/notifications`. The pending notifications direction, chosen earlier by the owner and recorded in `knowledge/analysis/notifications-rethink-2026-11-27.md` at `3f7eec8`, is not on main and is not implemented merely by restating it. R1a’s count changes and R4 wait until a notifications change merges, and R1/R4 must not temporarily publish misleading counts or erase email/export delivery behavior.
- **Focus/reflow:** a remounted page, conversation or provider iframe can lose drafts, read position, focus or the room connection. Keep owners stable and make layout CSS-driven where possible; tests must exercise the real resize and live-refetch paths.
- **Contrast:** the board checks six pairs only; derived surfaces, border strength, live colour and eight tutor colours may fail wider combinations. Preserve thresholds and seek an approved adjustment where token usage cannot satisfy both visual intent and accessibility.
- **Media support:** browser permissions vary and the provider has its own origin. Local preview cannot guarantee successful room media, and device streams can outlive the route if late promises are ignored. Real-media claims require evidence beyond the fake provider.
- **Provider limits:** no guarantee that BigBlueButton can reproduce either room drawing through supported configuration. Research can recommend a bounded approximation or a separately approved fork; it cannot promise exact parity.
- **Research/readiness:** the missing screen studies block implementation approval. External facts stay dated research, remote design services remain forbidden, and static mockup acceptance does not replace a rendered review of real synthetic-data screens.
