## 1. Problem, outcome and scope

**Problem.** OVC has relationships, groups, live updates and in-app notifications, but no lessons. A tutor cannot schedule a one-off lesson or a weekly series, nobody sees a calendar, no lesson emails exist, and the effects of ending a relationship, removing a provisional student, removing a group member, archiving a group and deleting a tutor stop short of lessons, which the slice 3 plan deferred here. No optional email exists yet, so the one-click unsubscribe headers, the dispatch-time preference check and minting unsubscribe tokens at render time, built in slice 4 as storage and routes only, have no consumer. One large tutor purge can still take a whole purge run's budget of batches and delay the purge of every other account by whole hourly runs; lessons, series and rosters make tutor purges larger.

**Outcome.** End to end through the real API, worker, PostgreSQL and the production frontend build:

- a tutor schedules a one-off lesson or a weekly series for one student (also a `provisional` one) or one group, in the tutor's timezone, with daylight-saving gaps and folds handled by fixed rules, and is warned before saving a lesson that overlaps another of theirs;
- a tutor edits or cancels one lesson, or this and all following lessons of a series, with the series split in place, and past, live and ended lessons stay as they were;
- tutors and students see their lessons in a week and month calendar (an agenda list on narrow screens) in their own profile timezone, with tutor labels and a tutor filter for students, and a lesson page with the second timezone when it differs;
- a lesson that nobody joined ends 30 minutes after its scheduled end as "did not take place" (joining arrives with video in slice 7);
- 24-hour and 1-hour reminders, `lesson.moved`, `lesson.cancelled`, `lesson.series_changed` and `group.archived` emails reach their recipients in their language and timezone, reflect the lesson as it is at sending, carry one-click unsubscribe headers and a visible unsubscribe link, and respect email preferences; the 1-hour reminder and the change events also produce in-app rows;
- ending a relationship, removing a provisional student, removing a group member, archiving a group and deleting a tutor cancel, end or narrow the affected lessons and series in their own transactions, and the `relationship.ended`, `group.archived` and `tutor.deleted` emails list the lessons they cancelled;
- the series horizon is extended daily, and long series never run out of occurrences while the worker runs;
- `entity.changed` with kind `lesson` keeps open calendars and lesson pages current;
- each account due for purge gets its own share of a purge run's budget, so one large tutor purge no longer delays the others by whole runs.

**In scope:**

- FR-4.1 to FR-4.5 in full; FR-4.6 without the `live` state and the tutor's ending (D1); FR-4.7 without materials, linked assignments, the join button and recordings (D1, D2);

- from slice 3: the `group.archived` email and in-app row; the lesson effects of ending a relationship, removing a provisional student, removing a group member, archiving a group and deleting a tutor, with the cancelled lessons listed in `relationship.ended` and `tutor.deleted`; one-to-one lessons only for a provisional student;
- from the slice 3 review: each account's own share of a purge run's budget;
- from the slice 4 plan: the `lesson` kind of `entity.changed`, the unsubscribe headers, the dispatch-time preference check and minting at render time;
- the frontend Schedule pages for both roles, the New lesson, edit and cancel dialogs with the overlap warning, the lesson page without video, lessons on the student card and the group page, the group archive confirmation, the in-app rows of the lesson events.

## 3. Decisions the plan proposes

Each names the alternative and the reason. S1 lands them in the specifications; `docs/` changes are marked pending owner review.

- **D1. No `live` state before slice 7.** Slice 5 implements `scheduled`, `cancelled` and `ended` without a tutor join. The status job ends every `scheduled` lesson 30 minutes after its scheduled end with its roster frozen, so every past lesson of slice 5 shows "Did not take place". The join, end and restart-room routes, `room_open`, `embed_allowed`, meetings and the status job's end-meeting call land in slice 7 with the provider; the lesson page shows the join states that need no join: before the window both roles see "Room opens at {time}"; inside the window the status word is "Starting soon", students see "The room opens when your tutor joins" and the tutor sees the time and status without a join area, because no join exists yet. *Alternative:* a placeholder join that only sets `live`. *Reason:* AGENTS.md forbids placeholder results, and `live` without a meeting would be a state the join flow never produces.
- **D2. Materials wait for the library.** `lesson_material`, `series_material`, the materials field of the dialogs and `POST /lessons/{id}/materials` land in slice 8, which creates the `material` table they reference; `overridden_fields` already accepts `materials`, and the split rules copy materials once they exist. *Reason:* a foreign key to a table that does not exist cannot be written, and an untyped identifier list would be a placeholder.
- **D3. Recurrence in one pure package.** `internal/domain/schedule` (pure, no I/O) computes occurrence dates from weekdays, first date and end, and each occurrence's start instant from its local date and the series' local start in the series timezone: a local time in a gap moves forward by the gap's length, and a repeated local time takes the earlier instant, both computed from the zone's transitions rather than from `time.Date`'s unspecified choice. Unit tests name both rules in two zones with daylight saving, a zone with a 30-minute shift, and a date on which a zone changed its standard offset. The server uses the host's zone database, with `time/tzdata` imported as the fallback when the host has none; the deployment pins its base image, so the database version is known, and the development guide records that a browser's zone data can differ from the server's, which affects only the display of instants in zones whose rules changed between the two versions. *Reason:* the specification and ADR-0007 require explicit rules and dedicated unit tests; the fallback removes a hard host dependency.
- **D4. Local input, server conversion.** `POST /lessons` takes a local date and local start time; the server converts them in the tutor's profile timezone at that moment (one-off) or stores them in the series timezone (series). The client never converts a local time to an instant for saving. The calendar's range bounds are the only instants the client computes, and a bound one day wider on each side is accepted (D24). *Alternative:* the client sends instants. *Reason:* the gap and fold rules live in one place.
- **D5. A `version` per lesson for conflicts.** Every lesson edit carries the `version` the client saw; it increments on every change of any edited field. `schedule_revision` keeps its role for reminder and `lesson.moved` keys and increments only on start or duration changes. An edit is a conditional update on `status = 'scheduled' AND starts_at > now AND version = <seen>` and loses with `not_editable` to a lesson that has left `scheduled` or started, and with `conflict` otherwise. *Alternative:* the specified condition on `schedule_revision`. *Reason:* two concurrent title edits would both pass a condition on a revision that titles do not change (K11).
- **D6. The edit body.** `PATCH /lessons/{id}` takes `{scope, version, date?, start?, duration_minutes?, title?, description?, allow_overlap?}`, with at least one field. `date` and `start` are local values in the lesson's timezone; `date` is refused for `scope: following` (`422 validation_failed`, `not_allowed`), because weekday changes are not offered; `scope: following` on a lesson without a series answers `422` (`not_allowed`). For "this and following", the selected occurrence takes every new value, as the split rules say; each following editable occurrence takes a changed field only where it has not overridden that field, and a new `start` applies on the occurrence's current local date (its moved date when it was moved). `POST /lessons/{id}/cancel` takes `{scope, version}`; a repeated cancellation of a lesson that is already `cancelled`, whatever its cause, answers `200` with the current state without checking `version`, and a repeated "cancel following" whose series already ends before the occurrence answers the current state likewise, as the API's idempotency convention requires. *Reason:* the specification lists the editable fields per scope but no body (K12).
- **D7. Splitting and cancelling following lock the series, then the lessons.** Split, "cancel following" and horizon extension lock the series row `FOR UPDATE` and re-read its end. The split ends the original series (an end date the day before the selected occurrence, a count converted into that end date, `ended_at` when no occurrence is left), inserts the new series with the original timezone, weekdays and `materialised_until`, its count equal to the covered occurrences (cancelled ones included) or the original end date, and moves the covered rows in place by `series_id`, keeping their identifiers. Covered rows are locked in identifier order after the series row. *Reason:* the specification fixes the outcome; the order makes the lock sequence explicit (D11).
- **D8. Cancellation records when and why.** `lesson` gains `cancelled_at` and `cancel_cause` (`tutor`, `relationship_ended`, `provisional_removed`, `group_archived`, `tutor_deleted`), set together and checked. The lesson page shows "Cancelled" with the cancellation date; the aggregated emails find their lessons by cause, subject and instant (D17). *Reason:* the lesson page shows the cancellation date, which no column holds, and an email that lists cancelled lessons needs to find them at dispatch from identifiers only (K5).
- **D9. Rosters freeze before the audience changes.** Every path that cancels or ends lessons because an audience changes cancels them first, freezing each roster from the audience at that moment, and then changes the relationship, the membership or the group: ending a relationship, archiving a group and tutor deletion. So cancelled lessons stay visible to the students who were expecting them, as the specification intends. For a provisional student's removal, a dropped reclaim link, the 7-day cleanup and the immediate purge of an unconfirmed account, the roster is frozen as Q2 decides. *Reason:* the audience of a one-to-one lesson is its active or provisional relationship, which no longer qualifies once ended, so freezing afterwards would give an empty roster (K6).
- **D10. Roster rows have no foreign key to the user.** `lesson_roster.student_id` is indexed but references nothing, as notification rows do; the student's purge deletes the account's roster rows through that index inside its batches. A roster row is inserted only while its transaction holds the student's relationship row with at least `FOR SHARE` and has re-checked its state; a student's purge discards those relationships before it deletes the roster rows and the user row, so an inserter waiting behind the purge finds the relationship discarded and inserts nothing. *Alternative:* a cascading foreign key. *Reason:* inserting a roster row with a key to the user takes `FOR KEY SHARE` on the student's user row, and tutor deletion, which freezes rosters, must lock no student's user row (accounts.md); roster rows reference their lesson with `ON DELETE CASCADE`.
- **D11. The lock order gains series and lessons, with an acquisition rule per path.** The order becomes: user rows, tutor profiles, relationships (identifier order), invitations, join requests, groups, series (identifier order), lessons (identifier order). Every path that names students (as audience, roster or frame recipients) holds their relationships with at least `FOR SHARE` before any group, series or lesson. Per path:
  - **creating, editing and cancelling** (tutor writes, D44): the tutor's profile, then the audience's relationships (`FOR SHARE`; for a group, its members read first without locks), then the group (`FOR SHARE`), then the series, then the lessons; the members read under the group lock must equal the locked set, otherwise the request retries once with the new set and then answers `409 conflict`, changing nothing;
  - **ending a relationship** (the tutor's or the student's): its existing locks, then the relationship's one-to-one series and lessons, and the group lessons from which the student leaves the audience are not locked (D37);
  - **removing a group member and changing members:** as today (relationships, then the group); no lesson row changes (D37);
  - **archiving a group:** the tutor's profile, the members' relationships (`FOR SHARE`, read first without locks), the group (`FOR UPDATE`), re-checking the members (retried once with the new set, then `409 conflict`, changing nothing), then the group's series and lessons; slice 3's profile-then-group order gains the relationships between the two;
  - **tutor deletion:** its existing locks (own user row, profile, every relationship, invitations, requests, groups), then the tutor's series and lessons;
  - **the worker jobs** (status, reminders, horizon extension): one transaction per lesson (per series for the extension); each reads the audience without locks, then locks the relationships (`FOR SHARE`), the group (`FOR SHARE`), the series and the lesson, re-reads the membership and the lesson's state, and handles the lesson only when the audience under the locks equals the one it locked; otherwise it retries once in the same run and leaves the lesson to the next run after that.

  *Reason:* the status job takes the lesson row lock (specification) and must name students, which would otherwise lock a relationship after a lesson, against the order of an ending; archiving must freeze rosters from its members (K19).
- **D12. The visibility of lessons is resolved in one place.** The repository exposes one query shape per role for "lessons this caller may see": a tutor sees their own lessons; a student sees `scheduled` lessons whose current audience includes them (a one-to-one lesson of their `active` or `provisional` relationship, a group lesson of a group they currently belong to) and every other lesson whose roster includes them. `GET /calendar`, `GET /lessons/{id}`, the student card, the group page and notification links use it; another tutor's lesson, a lesson of an ended relationship that is still scheduled, a lesson of a `reclaim_pending` or discarded relationship that is still scheduled, and a lesson of a group the student left answer `not_found`. An unconfirmed tutor has no lessons, because scheduling requires a confirmed tutor, so the calendar is empty for one. *Reason:* T-5, T-6 and T-21 depend on one rule; the specification's student calendar `UNION ALL` names only active relationships (K8).
- **D13. An unconfirmed student reaches the lessons of their provisional link.** ADR-0014 says a provisional link reaches its one-to-one lessons from slice 5. `GET /calendar` and `GET /lessons/{id}` therefore become `user, unverified ok`; for an unconfirmed student D12 yields the one-to-one lessons of the provisional relationship and, after Q2, nothing of an earlier discarded link (an unconfirmed account belongs to no group and has no other relationship), and navigation shows Schedule to an unconfirmed student whose `GET /tutors` lists a provisional relationship. The routes write nothing, so the session re-check rule does not apply. *Alternative:* the routes stay `user` and the student sees nothing until confirming. *Reason:* the decision record governs the specification (K40); T-44 is amended for what a squatting account then sees: the times and titles of the lessons the tutor scheduled for that email's owner, until the removal or the reclaim.
- **D14. Per-tutor caps.** A tutor has at most a capped number of series that have not ended (`ended_at` is null and the end date or the last counted occurrence is not past) and of future `scheduled` lessons, with the numbers of Q4; creating or splitting beyond either answers `422 validation_failed` (`body`, `too_many`), counted under the tutor's profile lock at creation and split. Horizon extension is exempt, because it only keeps each series 12 weeks ahead; the bound on future lessons is therefore the lesson cap plus, for each series, at most 7 × 13 materialised occurrences, which section 9 measures. *Reason:* without a cap one account can create unbounded rows and make every overlap check, deletion and purge of that tutor unbounded (T-20); an extension that stopped at the cap would let series silently run out of occurrences.
- **D15. Overlap response is bounded.** `409 lesson_overlap` carries at most 20 conflicting lessons (title, start, end), earliest first, and `more` with the number left out. The check expands both sides virtually up to each series' end (bounded by D14), loads the tutor's lessons and not-ended series in the affected period in one range query each, and sweeps in memory. *Reason:* an 18-month series against 300 series could otherwise return thousands of items (K10).
- **D16. Idempotent creation.** `POST /lessons` stores the `Idempotency-Key` per tutor with the created row: `lesson.create_key` for a one-off lesson and `lesson_series.create_key` for a series, each unique with `tutor_id` where not null; a repeated key returns the first result, also when the body differs, as for cards and groups; the key is never logged. *Reason:* the API conventions require it; the specification names no column (K28).
- **D17. Aggregated emails find their lessons at dispatch.** The payloads stay identifiers only:
  - `relationship.ended` lists the lessons with `cancel_cause = 'relationship_ended'`, that relationship and `cancelled_at` equal to its `ended_at`;
  - `group.archived` the lessons with `cancel_cause = 'group_archived'`, that group and `cancelled_at` equal to its `archived_at`, whose roster includes the recipient;
  - `tutor.deleted` the lessons with `cancel_cause = 'tutor_deleted'`, that tutor and `cancelled_at` equal to its `deleted_at`, whose roster includes the recipient;
  - `lesson.series_changed` carries the operation identifier and the identifiers of the affected lessons (at most 100 per series operation, bounded by the series rules) and lists those whose roster or current audience includes the recipient at dispatch.

  `cancelled_at` is written in the same transaction from the value the ending, archiving or deletion statement stored (read back with `RETURNING`, at the column's microsecond precision), never from a second clock read, so the equality holds; a relationship reactivated and ended again gets a new `ended_at` and lists only its own cancellations. Each email lists at most 20 lessons in start order and then "and {count} more". *Alternative:* lesson lists in payloads for every aggregated email. *Reason:* the cause and instant already identify the set, and the payload rule stays as the specification has it; a cap keeps a tutor deletion's email readable (K13).
- **D18. In-app rows of the lesson events.** The notification `event` check widens with `lesson.reminder_1h`, `lesson.moved`, `lesson.cancelled`, `lesson.series_changed` and `group.archived`. `subject_id` holds the lesson (for `lesson.series_changed` the occurrence the tutor acted on) or the group; `student_id` is the student recipient, or for the tutor's reminder the student of a one-to-one lesson and `null` for a group lesson; `relationship_id` gives students the tutor colour. Labels resolve at read: the lesson's title while the lesson is visible to the caller (D12), and for `group.archived` the group's label under D42, `null` otherwise. `link` gains the kind `lesson` (`{kind: lesson, id}` while the lesson is visible to the caller); `group.archived` has no link. Keys are the email keys of the event table. *Reason:* the slice 4 row schema names only relationships and join requests (K3).
- **D19. Titles and descriptions are plain text.** A title holds 1 to 120 characters, a description at most 2,000, both as entered; the interface renders them as text with line breaks kept, emails escape them in HTML and collapse whitespace in plain text, and subjects and headers remove CR and LF, drop bidirectional controls and zero-width characters, and use RFC 2047; line breaks in descriptions are kept with CSS (`white-space: pre-line`), never through an HTML sink. Restricted Markdown arrives with the builder's renderer in slice 8 if a later decision asks for it. *Reason:* the specification gives neither format nor bounds (K14), and no renderer exists before slice 8 (T-14).
- **D20. The default title.** The New lesson dialog prefills the title from "Lesson" and the audience's name as Q5 decides, editable before saving; the server requires a title and stores what it receives. *Reason:* a server-built default would hide from the tutor what the students see.
- **D21. Participants on the lesson page.** `GET /lessons/{id}` has one schema for both roles, because one path cannot carry two response schemas (as decided for `GET /notifications` in slice 4), and every field's value per role is fixed by the label table of D42: `audience` is `{kind, label, colour, members}`, `tutor` is `{name, colour}` for students and `null` for the tutor. A contract test proves that no student response carries a card display name, a member list (unless Q3 says otherwise) or a tutor-only field. *Reason:* FR-4.7's "participants" and pages.md's "audience" disagree on what students see (K17).
- **D22. The second timezone.** `GET /lessons/{id}` returns `other_timezone` or `null`: the student's profile timezone for the tutor of a one-to-one lesson when it differs from the tutor's, the lesson's tutor-side timezone for a student when it differs from the student's, `null` for the tutor of a group lesson. The client renders `time.other_zone` from it.
- **D23. One timezone city table, two copies.** The city names in the form `time.other_zone` needs (English, and the Russian genitive) for every canonical IANA zone that browsers report live in `frontend/src/lib/time/timezone-cities.json` and in `backend/internal/core/timezonecities/timezone-cities.json`; `TestTimezoneCityTablesAreIdentical` compares the two byte for byte, and a frontend unit test checks that every zone of `Intl.supportedValuesOf('timeZone')` in the test engine has an entry; any other zone the server accepts falls back to the `UTC±hh:mm` form under a second key `time.other_offset`, which a backend test covers for a zone missing from the table. The frontend table loads only with the lesson page's chunk. *Alternative:* one file imported across the two trees. *Reason:* `go:embed` cannot reach outside the Go module, and the frontend build should not import from the backend tree; emails need the same city names (pilot scenario 1, step 7).
- **D24. Calendar route.** `GET /calendar?from=&to=&tutor=` takes RFC 3339 instants, `to − from` at most 62 days plus one day of margin on each side (D4), `tutor` only for students (a tutor sending it gets `422`, `not_allowed`) and only the identifier of a relationship that the student's My tutors lists (`active`, `provisional` or `ended`); any other identifier, including a discarded or `reclaim_pending` one, answers `404` like a random one; items `{id, title, starts_at, ends_at, status, timezone, audience: {kind, label, colour}, tutor: {name, colour} | null}`, labelled by D42, at most 1,000 per response with `truncated: true` beyond, ordered by start; `no-store`. *Reason:* the specification gives the bounds but no item schema, no filter refusal and no item cap (K25, K26).
- **D25. `entity.changed lesson` recipients.** The tutor on every committed change of the lesson; each student of the current audience (for a scheduled lesson) or of the roster (otherwise) when a field they see changes (time, duration, title, description, status), within the slice 4 rules: recipients named under the relationship lock (D11), never another tutor of the same student, and the acting account's other sockets. Ending a relationship, a removal and leaving a group send the student who lost the lesson one frame per affected lesson only when that lesson stays visible to them (a frozen roster), and nothing for lessons they no longer see, which they lose on their next fetch. A series operation sends one frame per affected lesson, chunked under the 100-recipient limit. *Reason:* the slice 4 table has no `lesson` row (K19).
- **D26. Reminder and status jobs' budgets.** The reminder job selects lessons 50 at a time through the partial `(starts_at)` index for scheduled lessons and handles each in its own transaction (D11), within a 20-second budget per run; the status job ends up to 500 lessons per run, oldest scheduled end first, each in its own transaction; horizon extension handles up to 200 series per run, each series in its own transaction, resuming from the series with the oldest `materialised_until`. *Reason:* the specification bounds the reminder job only (K29), and one transaction per lesson keeps D11's order.
- **D27. The first optional emails.** Optional emails (`lesson_reminders`, `lesson_changes`) carry `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and a visible "Turn off these emails" link to `/unsubscribe?token=…` in both bodies, with one token minted per send at render time through the slice 4 signer, never stored, never in a payload and never logged. The dispatcher checks the recipient's preference group in the batched recipient query and marks the row `done` with `obsolete` when it is off. The mail sink gains a `headers` column (S3's migration) and stores the headers and links as sent, so tests read the token from the sink. Each minted token names the recipient's own account and the email's group, which a test asserts. The `Message-ID` stays derived from the outbox idempotency key: an unsubscribe token has no identifier, so a retried reminder keeps its identifier although its token is minted again. DKIM signing is the provider's; the local sink has none, and the deployment checks in slice 10 confirm that the provider signs both headers. *Reason:* notifications.md defers exactly these to the first optional email.
- **D28. Plural rules without a dependency.** The CLDR cardinal rules for Russian (`one`, `few`, `many`, `other`) and English (`one`, `other`) are hand-written in `internal/core`, with unit tests over the boundary numbers (0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 11, 12, 14, 21, 22, 25, 101, 111), and used by the email catalogue. *Alternative:* `golang.org/x/text`'s plural package. *Reason:* two languages need about twenty lines; a dependency needs a stack-table row and scanning (AGENTS.md).
- **D29. Dates in emails.** Emails show the lesson's start in the recipient's profile timezone and language (Russian month names in the genitive), with the city of D23 when the recipient's zone differs from the lesson's tutor-side zone, through a hand-written catalogue in `internal/core`. *Reason:* FR-13.4 and NFR-5.3.
- **D30. Purge budget per account.** A run of `accounts.purge` (and of `accounts.cleanup_unverified`) gives the due accounts its 100 batch transactions round-robin: each round gives every account still due one batch, oldest deletion first, until the budget is spent or no account is due. `account.purge_paused` reports the batches each account spent. The tutor purge's batch kinds gain lesson rosters, lessons and series (newest series first, so a series is deleted before the series it was split from; `split_from_series_id` is `ON DELETE SET NULL` as well), each 500 rows at a time in key order, before group members; the student purge gains the account's roster rows. *Alternative:* rounds of ten batches, or a fixed share of 100 divided by the accounts due. *Reason:* with one batch per round even many large accounts cannot hold back a small one by a whole run; the slice 3 review carried this item.
- **D31. ## 9. Risks, performance and the check budget

**Risks.**

- **Timezone rules:** subtle errors around gaps and folds; the unit tests of D3 name each case, and the frontend shows only what the server computed.
- **Lock order:** jobs that name students must lock relationships first (D11); the `pg_locks` tests of S3, S4 and S5 guard it, and the slice 3 race tests are extended.
- **Large transactions:** tutor deletion now cancels every future lesson of the tutor (at most about 12,100 with Q4 A) and freezes their rosters (up to 30 rows per group lesson) in one transaction; measured below against a declared budget of 5 seconds at that bound. Above it, the fallback is cancelling in the deletion's transaction and freezing rosters in the first purge batches, which needs a specification change and an owner question.
- **Reminder bursts:** many lessons starting at one minute (for example on the hour) produce one outbox row per recipient; the job's budget and the per-lane loops bound the run, and NFR-4.5 is measured below.
- **Email headers:** tutor-written titles in subjects (T-31); tested in S3.
- **The owner's `backend/.env`** needs nothing new: the unsubscribe secret exists since slice 4.
- **Dependency on the decision about minors.** The owner decided on 2026-10-25 that students may be minors and that the pilot runs with tutors of school students; the decision record is not written yet, and until it is the documented scope stays adult students and no feature collects an age or a guardian's data. Slice 5 collects no new personal data: lessons hold the tutor's titles and times, reminders go to the student's own address, and nothing depends on age. What could change once the decision record lands: copies of reminders or change emails to a legal guardian (a guardian recipient would join the recipient resolution of S3 and S4, which stays in one function per event for that reason), quieter hours for emails to minors (none of slice 5's emails is sent at a time the tutor did not choose, since reminders follow lesson times), and school-term series (a series spanning a holiday is cancelled lesson by lesson; a holiday pause is a proposal after the MVP). School students' lessons cluster in the afternoons and evenings of school days, which makes reminder bursts at round hours more likely; the NFR-4.5 measurement below uses such a burst.

**Performance measurements at the slice review,** serially on an idle machine, recorded in `knowledge/analysis/`:

- `GET /calendar` for a tutor with 300 series and 3,000 future lessons, and for a student with five tutors and three groups, at 62 days: p95;
- the overlap check of an 18-month, seven-weekday series against 300 series: duration and memory;
- `POST /lessons` with 100 occurrences, and a split of a 100-lesson series;
- tutor deletion at the bound of Q4 (for A, 100 series materialised 12 weeks ahead on seven weekdays and 3,000 one-off lessons, half of them group lessons of 30): budget 5 s, against the slice 3 baseline of 0.95 s at 50,000 relationships;
- the status and reminder jobs with 500 lessons starting in the same minute, 100 of them group lessons of 30: run time and outbox rows;
- NFR-4.5: a 1-hour reminder due while 1,000 other emails are queued with a 200 ms send each: time until it is sent (target: under 2 minutes);
- a purge run with one tutor needing 300 batches and 20 small accounts: when each small account is purged;
- frontend: the Schedule page's chunk and first render of a month with 300 lessons, 100 week navigations and 100 dialog open-and-close cycles with flat listeners, DOM nodes and query-cache entries;
- the full local check run, cold, on an idle machine.
