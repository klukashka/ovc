## 1. Problem, outcome and scope

**Problem.** Slice 2 built tutor accounts, sessions, email and purge, but a tutor cannot reach a student: there are no student cards, invitations, tutor codes, join requests, relationships or groups; `POST /auth/register` refuses `role: student`; `/invite/{token}`, `/students`, `/groups`, `/tutors` and `/settings/teaching` are placeholders; the API has no role audiences, so no real route answers `403 email_not_verified`; `POST /auth/verify-email` always returns `outcome: null`; and deletion knows nothing about relationships. The owner accepted [proposal 0002](../../docs/proposals/0002-student-onboarding.md) on 2026-10-22, which replaces the onboarding model of the specifications and of ADR-0009 item 4.

**Outcome.** End to end through the real API, worker, PostgreSQL, storage and the production frontend build:

- a confirmed tutor creates a student card, copies its link or has OVC email it, and regenerates, withdraws or edits it;
- a person opens the link, sees the tutor, signs up in place (or signs in and accepts) and is linked at once, provisionally until the email is confirmed; the tutor can remove a provisional student and gets a fresh card;
- a mailbox owner who reclaims an unconfirmed account explicitly keeps or drops each provisional link and held request;
- a tutor shares and regenerates a tutor code; a student enters it at sign-up or later; the tutor accepts, declines, or declines and blocks; requests from unconfirmed accounts are held; unanswered requests expire after 14 days;
- a tutor ends a relationship from the student card, and a student ends it from My tutors ("Stop studying"), with the same effects and the `relationship.ended` email to the other party; a new invitation or an accepted request reactivates it;
- a tutor creates groups from active students, renames, recolours, adds and removes members, archives and unarchives;
- a student sees their tutors, active and past, with colours, on My tutors and Home;
- deleting or purging a tutor or a student applies its effects to relationships, cards, invitations, join requests and groups, and a tutor's deletion sends `tutor.deleted`;
- every scoped route resolves identifiers within the caller's relationships and answers `not_found` outside them, proved by integration tests over the real API.

**In scope:**

- FR-1.1 confirm-before-inviting; FR-1.2 rewritten (invitation or code); FR-1.3 role mismatch; FR-1.7 effects on relationships, groups, cards, invitations and join requests, with `tutor.deleted`;
- FR-2.1 to FR-2.7 without the parts that need lessons, work, chat or recordings, and the new FR-2.8 (a student ends a relationship, pending owner review);
- FR-3.1, FR-3.3 and FR-3.4 for membership and archive state; FR-3.2 only as the group entity;
- role audiences and the `email_not_verified` gate on real routes;
- the confirmation outcome in `POST /auth/verify-email` and `POST /auth/password/reset`, and the reclaim choice;
- emails `invitation.created`, `relationship.ended` (to the student or the tutor), `tutor.deleted`;
- worker jobs `join_requests.expire`, `invitations.expire` and `reclaim.expire`; the purge and unverified-cleanup jobs extended to the new tables;
- pages `/invite/{token}`, `/signup/student` (code form), `/reclaim` (the choice page), `/students`, `/students/{id}`, `/groups`, `/groups/{id}`, `/tutors`, `/settings/teaching` (code and blocked students only), the join-request panel and the code on Today, the tutor parts of student Home, and the unconfirmed-student banner;
- the carried-over test fix: the purge race test settles its upload goroutine before the test database is dropped, also after a failure.

## 3. Decisions (owner, 2026-10-22)

- **D1.** Proposal 0002 is accepted. ADR-0014 "Students join through their tutor" replaces the first bullet of ADR-0009 item 4 (an emailed invitation no longer confirms the email) and adds the `provisional` state and the removal transition to ADR-0005.
- **D2.** The provisional link is an explicit relationship state `provisional`; at most one provisional relationship per student account (partial unique index), which is also why an unconfirmed account cannot hold a second tutor's link.
- **D3.** Five `invitation_email_mismatch` refusals on one invitation revoke it; the card shows "Link revoked after repeated wrong emails"; later attempts get the ordinary invalid answer. Residual: a link holder can revoke a leaked link on purpose.
- **D4. Reclaim: the mailbox owner chooses** (replaces the earlier reclaim options). A reclaim is a confirmation with a new password from a browser not signed in to the account, or a password reset of an `unverified` account. Its transaction confirms the email and moves every provisional relationship to `reclaim_pending` and every held request to `held` with `owner_choice_pending`; nothing becomes permanent, and no request becomes visible to a tutor, without an explicit keep. The owner then sees each item (tutor name, avatar and subjects) and keeps or drops it: keeping a link makes it `active`; dropping it applies the removal effects (D6), so the tutor gets a new card; keeping a request releases it under D7; dropping it expires it silently. An ordinary confirmation from the browser signed in to the account (its creator) keeps the D7 behaviour without a choice. Unchosen items are dropped 7 days after the reclaim (D27). The full `reclaim_pending` rules are in 4.5.
- **D5. A student ends a relationship themselves** ("Stop studying" on My tutors), with the same effects as an ending by the tutor, and the `relationship.ended` email goes to the other party, the tutor. New FR-2.8, pending owner review. Either party can reactivate it only through the existing paths (a new invitation accepted by the same account, or a join request accepted by the tutor).
- **D6.** Removal of a provisional student uses `POST /students/{id}/remove`, allowed only while `provisional`, answering `409 relationship_not_provisional` otherwise; the card's "End" action calls it. Removal is silent, the removed account no longer lists the tutor, the old row is discarded with its `notes` and `email` cleared before the new card carrying them is inserted (the partial unique index on tutor and email would otherwise refuse it), the discarded row is invisible to the tutor, and from slice 5 future one-to-one lessons are cancelled without an email. The 7-day cleanup, the immediate purge of an unverified account and a dropped reclaim link apply the same effects. A card created by removal does not count toward the card limit.
- **D7.** A held request is released at confirmation (and on a reclaim keep): it becomes visible `pending` when the tutor is `active` and has fewer than 50 visible pending requests; it is `suppressed` when the tutor has blocked the student or the limit is full; it expires when the tutor is not `active`. The 14-day expiry runs from creation on the injected clock. A suppressed request for the limit becomes visible, oldest first, in the transaction that frees a slot; a suppressed request for a block expires when the tutor unblocks the student, so the student can send a fresh one.
- **D8.** `POST /auth/register` accepts `role: student` only with `tutor_code`; `/signup/student` is the code form.
- **D9.** A tutor entering a code or accepting an invitation gets `409 role_mismatch`; every other role-specific route answers `not_found` to the other role.
- **D10.** One open invitation per card, copied and emailed alike; stored as a SHA-256 hash for lookup and AES-256-GCM ciphertext (standard library; additional data: the invitation identifier) under `OVC_FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY`; the ciphertext is set to null once the invitation is used, revoked or expired; the email key is `(invitation.created, invitation, send_count)`.
- **D11.** The invitation email uses the language of the account that holds the card's email, otherwise the tutor's; the tutor's name appears only in the body, as escaped plain text, never as a link or in the subject.
- **D12.** Card limits: 50 new cards and 50 card email changes per tutor per day.
- **D13.** An unconfirmed student sees a confirmation banner and reaches Home, My tutors and Settings; every other private address redirects to `/verify-email`. (`/reclaim` concerns only confirmed accounts, D4.)
- **D14.** The confirmation outcome stays one value, chosen by precedence: `choice_required` (reclaim items pending), then `linked`, `request_sent`, `code_invalid` (a held request expired at release), else `null`. `invitation_invalid` leaves the enum. `POST /auth/password/reset` answers only `choice_required` or `null`: a reset is either a reclaim or concerns an active account, which has no provisional link or held request.
- **D15.** No in-app notification rows in slice 3; slice 4 adds the table, the routes and the relationship producers.
- **D16.** Today gets the join-request panel and the tutor code, Home gets "No tutor yet / Request sent" and My tutors; both read existing routes; `GET /home` lands in slice 10.
- **D17.** `group.archived` waits for slice 5.
- **D18.** `relationship.ended` and `tutor.deleted` are sent now without a lesson list; `relationship.ended` is obsolete when the relationship was reactivated, `tutor.deleted` when the tutor is no longer `deleted`.
- **D19.** Invitation expiry needs no state change for acceptance (read from `expires_at`), but the hourly `invitations.expire` job clears the ciphertext of expired invitations through a partial index (D10), which keeps the specification's job.
- **D20.** Tutor deletion discards provisional relationships without a new card, and their unconfirmed students get no `tutor.deleted` email.
- **D21.** Correcting a mistyped unconfirmed email is deferred.
- **D22.** The code is 8 characters from `23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTUVWXYZ`, shown as `XXXX-XXXX`, matched case-insensitively with spaces and hyphens ignored; minted at tutor sign-up and backfilled by the migration, retried on a unique violation; regeneration is limited to 10 per tutor per day.
- **D23.** `GET /join-requests` serves tutors and `GET /join-requests/sent` serves students. One path cannot carry two response schemas in huma's typed registration, and the API rule keeps tutor and student structs separate wherever their content differs; the student rows carry state and times only.
- **D24.** `Idempotency-Key` is stored as a nullable `create_key` with a unique `(tutor_id, create_key)` index on `relationships` and `groups`.
- **D25.** Invitations and code entry require the tutor to be `active`: a blocked or deleted tutor's link answers `invitation_invalid` and their code `tutor_code_invalid` (T-22).
- **D26.** The per-recipient invitation cap is silent: only the tutor's own daily budget answers `429`; over the recipient cap, or for an address whose account is blocked or deleted, the route answers `202` and drops the send; the card never shows delivery status.
- **D27.** Reclaim items the owner never chooses are dropped 7 days after the reclaim by the hourly `reclaim.expire` job: links with the removal effects (the tutor gets a new card), held requests expired.
- **D28.** "Stop studying" on a provisional link is allowed and applies the removal effects silently (the tutor gets a new card); a provisional link never becomes `ended`.
- **D29.** No `student_name_snapshot`: after a student purge the tutor's history shows the tutor's own display name for the card; the column leaves the data model.
- **D30.** At most 200 groups per tutor, archived groups included; the 201st is refused with `422 validation_failed`, so `GET /groups` stays unpaginated and bounded.
- **D31.** When a tutor accepts a join request and no relationship or matching invited card exists, the new card's `display_name` is a tutor-owned copy of the student's account name taken at accept time, which the tutor can edit; its `email` stays null. Residual recorded in T-31 (student-written text reaches the tutor's `relationship.ended` email, escaped) and T-34 (the copy survives the student's purge until the tutor's purge); D29's "no snapshot" stays true for every other path.
- **D32.** The role check comes before any validation of the body. A caller of the other role always gets `404 not_found`, whatever the body holds: missing required fields, wrong lengths or formats, or JSON that is not well formed. The order after the role check is set by D33 and D34.
- **D33.** The `403 email_not_verified` refusal follows the role check directly and also comes before the body is read. The order is therefore: role `404`, `403`, parsing (`400`, `422`), scope `404`, `409`, then the remaining `422`.
- **D34.** From S6 on, huma's schema validation of the body (required fields, lengths, patterns, types) answers `422 validation_failed` before scope resolution. It depends only on the request body and reveals nothing about other tutors' data. Field rules that need scope or state (for example a duplicate email) stay in the service, after scope and `409`.

### 4.2 Data model (migration `00004_tutoring.sql`, one change, goose markers only)

- **`tutor_profiles.code`** `text NOT NULL UNIQUE CHECK (code ~ '^[2-9A-HJKMNP-Z]{8}$')`; the migration backfills existing rows (development and test data only, since nothing is deployed) from `gen_random_uuid()` bytes over the alphabet, failing loudly on a collision; new tutors get a code at sign-up, retried on a unique violation.
- **`users.reclaim_until`** `timestamptz NULL`: the deadline of a reclaim, written only by the account's own transactions: set once by the reclaim (which already holds the account's row) to the reclaim time plus 7 days, and cleared by the owner's choice, by a signed-in acceptance that keeps the last pending link, by the account's deletion, or lazily by `reclaim.expire` when no item is pending or the deadline has passed. Tutor paths (removal, tutor deletion) never write it, so they never lock a student's user row for it; it is only a hint for the job, and whether a choice is due is derived from the pending rows (4.4). A partial index where not null serves `reclaim.expire`. No other user column (D1 removes the pending code and reservation).
- **`relationships`**: `id`; `tutor_id` → `tutor_profiles(user_id) ON DELETE CASCADE`; `student_id` → `users(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT` (purge clears it first); `state` in (`invited`, `provisional`, `reclaim_pending`, `active`, `ended`, `discarded`); `discard_reason` in (`withdrawn`, `merged`, `removed`, `tutor_deleted`, `student_purged`); `ended_by` in (`tutor`, `student`, `deletion`); `display_name` 1–100; `email citext` 3–254 nullable; `colour` in the eight palette keys; `notes` ≤ 10,000 default `''`; `create_key`; `linked_at`, `ended_at`, `discarded_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`. Checks: `invited` ⇒ `student_id` null; `provisional`, `reclaim_pending`, `active`, `ended` ⇒ `student_id` not null; `discarded` either; `discard_reason` set exactly when `discarded`; `ended_at` and `ended_by` set when `ended`, kept when a student purge discards an ended row, and null otherwise. Indexes: unique `(tutor_id, student_id)` where `student_id IS NOT NULL AND state <> 'discarded'`; unique `(tutor_id, email)` where `email IS NOT NULL AND state <> 'discarded'`; unique `(student_id)` where `state = 'provisional'` (D2); `(tutor_id, state, created_at DESC, id DESC)`; `(student_id)`; unique `(tutor_id, create_key)` where not null. No `student_name_snapshot` (D29).
- **`invitations`**: `id`; `relationship_id` → `relationships ON DELETE CASCADE`; `token_hash bytea UNIQUE` (32 bytes); `token_encrypted bytea NULL`; `bound_student_id` → `users ON DELETE CASCADE`; `expires_at`; `used_at`; `used_by` → `users ON DELETE SET NULL`; `revoked_at`; `revoke_reason` in (`regenerated`, `withdrawn`, `email_changed`, `mismatch_limit`, `linked`, `removed`, `tutor_deleted`, `expired_on_send`); `mismatch_count smallint` 0–5; `send_count smallint`; `created_at`. Checks: `token_encrypted` null whenever `used_at` or `revoked_at` is set. Indexes: unique `(relationship_id)` where `used_at IS NULL AND revoked_at IS NULL`; `(relationship_id)`; `(bound_student_id)`; `(used_by)`; partial `(expires_at)` where `token_encrypted IS NOT NULL`.
- **`join_requests`**: `id`; `tutor_id` → `tutor_profiles ON DELETE CASCADE`; `student_id` → `users ON DELETE CASCADE`; `state` in (`held`, `pending`, `accepted`, `declined`, `expired`); `suppressed` in (`none`, `blocked`, `limit`); `owner_choice_pending boolean`; `expires_at`, `decided_at`, `created_at`. Checks: `owner_choice_pending` only while `held`; `suppressed <> 'none'` only while `pending`. Indexes: unique `(tutor_id, student_id)` where `state IN ('held', 'pending')`; partial `(expires_at)` where `state IN ('held', 'pending')`; partial `(tutor_id, created_at, id)` where `state = 'pending' AND suppressed = 'none'` (the tutor list and the limit count); partial `(tutor_id, created_at, id)` where `state = 'pending' AND suppressed = 'limit'` (un-suppression); `(tutor_id)`; `(student_id, created_at DESC, id DESC)`.
- **`blocked_requesters`**: `tutor_id`, `student_id` (both cascade), `created_at`; primary key on the pair; `(tutor_id, created_at DESC, student_id)`; `(student_id)`.
- **`groups`**: `id`; `tutor_id` → `tutor_profiles ON DELETE CASCADE`; `name` 1–60; `colour`; `archived_at`; `create_key`; `created_at`, `updated_at`; `(tutor_id)`; unique `(tutor_id, create_key)`.
- **`group_members`**: `group_id`, `relationship_id`, `tutor_id`, `added_at`; `(group_id, tutor_id)` → `groups (id, tutor_id) ON DELETE CASCADE` and `(relationship_id, tutor_id)` → `relationships (id, tutor_id) ON DELETE CASCADE`, backed by unique `(id, tutor_id)` on both parent tables, so a group can never hold another tutor's relationship; primary key on `(group_id, relationship_id)`; `(relationship_id)`.
- **Purge.** Student purge: clears `student_id` and `email` on every relationship row of the student, discarded ones included, and discards the non-discarded ones (`student_purged`), keeping `ended_at` and `ended_by`; a provisional or reclaim-pending link of a purged unverified account is discarded with the removal effects (D6); deletes their memberships, join requests, blocked-requester rows and bound invitations; `used_by` falls to null. Tutor purge: runs in batches of 500 rows per transaction (each locking the user row and re-checking `deleted`), deleting group members, groups, invitations, join requests, blocked requesters and relationships, then the final transaction deletes the profile and the user; every cascading foreign key is indexed.
- **sqlc queries** in `queries/relationships.sql`, `invitations.sql`, `join_requests.sql`, `groups.sql` and `users.sql`; every list is keyset ordered; every scoped read takes `tutor_id` or `student_id` as a parameter; groups per card and the first five members per group are each one batched query (`= ANY($ids)` with a window function), never one query per row.

### 4.3 Backend packages

- **`internal/domain`**: `RelationshipID`, `InvitationID`, `JoinRequestID`, `GroupID`; enums for relationship states, discard, revoke and ending reasons, join-request states and suppression, palette colours, link outcomes; pure rules, unit-tested: the relationship state machine (including reclaim and student ending), colour choice (the first palette key unused among the student's non-discarded relationships, then the least used, in palette order), code alphabet and normalisation, the outcome precedence; error code `relationship_not_provisional` (409); outbox kinds; jobs `join_requests.expire`, `invitations.expire`, `reclaim.expire`.
- **`internal/core`**: `FieldCipher` (AES-256-GCM, 12-byte random nonce, additional data the invitation identifier) from `OVC_FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (32 bytes, base64, a `Secret`), required by the API and the worker; invitation token minting (32 random bytes, base64url) and hashing; email text catalogue for the three emails.
- **`internal/repositories`**: `Relationships`, `Invitations`, `JoinRequests`, `BlockedRequesters`, `Groups`, and code methods on `TutorProfiles`. Atomic statements: invitation consumption (`used_at IS NULL AND revoked_at IS NULL AND expires_at > now`, setting the ciphertext null); the mismatch counter, one `UPDATE … SET mismatch_count = mismatch_count + 1, revoked_at = CASE WHEN mismatch_count + 1 >= 5 THEN $now END, revoke_reason = …, token_encrypted = CASE … END WHERE id = $1 AND used_at IS NULL AND revoked_at IS NULL RETURNING mismatch_count`; removal (`state = 'provisional'`); request decisions (`state = 'pending'`).
- **`internal/services/tutoring`**: cards, invitations, the invitation page, signed-in acceptance, join requests, ending by either party, reactivation, groups, blocked requesters, the code. It implements `accounts.TutoringEffects`, defined in `internal/services/accounts` so the import stays one-way:

  The prechecks run before any password is hashed and outside the transaction (token usable, tutor active, card email binding, code valid); the transactional methods re-check under locks. The implementation is injected into `accounts.Service`, `accounts.Purger` and `services/operators` by `cmd/internal/startup`.
- **Lock order** (stated in the accounts specification): a transaction that needs user rows locks all of them up front, in one statement `SELECT … FROM users WHERE id = ANY($ids) ORDER BY id FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, re-checks their states, and never locks another user row later; then every tutor profile row it needs (for the 50-pending cap, code regeneration, un-suppression and the group cap), all up front in identifier order, never another profile later (release at confirmation, student deletion and un-suppression can touch several tutors); then relationships in identifier order, then invitations, then join requests, then groups. Every path that frees or fills a pending slot follows it, including the jobs: decline, accept, `join_requests.expire`, student deletion, release at confirmation, un-suppression and invitation acceptance (signed in or through a merge, which can accept a visible pending request and so frees a slot) lock the student's user row and then the tutor profiles before touching relationships and requests; `reclaim.expire` locks the user row first, exactly as the owner's choice does, so the two serialise. Tutor deletion locks only the tutor's own user row and profile (as slice 2's `DeleteAccount` does) and then relationships, invitations, requests and groups, never a student's user row; its discard and revoke steps are statements issued after those locks, so they also see cards and invitations that a concurrent owner's drop, `reclaim.expire` or removal committed while it waited; tutor removal locks the student's row and then the relationship; so no two transactions wait on each other's rows in a cycle, which `TestTutorDeletionRacingTheOwnersReclaimChoiceHasOneOutcome` and `TestSlotFreeingPathsFollowTheLockOrder` prove.
- **Session re-check under the lock.** A request is authenticated once at its start, while confirmation, reset and reclaim revoke sessions inside their own transaction, so a request authenticated before a reclaim could wait on the user row and act after it. One rule therefore covers every write open to unconfirmed accounts and every transaction that acts for a signed-in student: all of `PATCH /users/me`, the avatar upload's row creation and settling, `PUT /users/me/email-preferences` when it lands in slice 4, an unverified tutor's writes to the tutor profile and settings, the library, and library and prompt-image uploads as they land, signed-in acceptance, code entry, `POST /tutors/{relationshipId}/end`, the same-browser confirmation path, the reclaim choice, and any later write with that audience. Each goes through one helper, `lockActingAccount`. It locks the user row, then reads the session row in a separate statement (under READ COMMITTED a new statement sees a revocation committed while it waited, whereas a single join locking only `users` would keep the stale session row), and re-checks that the session is not revoked and that the account is still in the state the request was authorised for (for example `unverified` for code entry by an unconfirmed account); otherwise it answers `401 unauthenticated` and changes nothing. A squatter's name or avatar edit therefore cannot land after a reclaim, and the `/reclaim` page shows the account's current name and avatar with a link to edit them before anything is kept. Password change and account deletion are already guarded by the password-hash comparison under the row lock. The accounts specification states this rule beside the lock order.
- **Concurrent acceptances** by one account are serialised by its user row lock, and concurrent acceptances of one invitation by the conditional consumption, so neither the one-provisional-link index nor the tutor-and-student index can surface a unique violation as a `500`.
- **`internal/services/accounts`**: `Register` with a student code (D8: per-IP request slot first, then `PrecheckCode`, then validation and hashing); `RegisterThroughInvitation` (per-IP slot, `PrecheckInvitation`, hashing in every remaining case, insert-if-free with `LinkAtSignUp`, or the duplicate notice); `ConfirmEmail` and `ResetPassword` call `OnEmailConfirmed` (reclaim when the new-password path or a reset of an `unverified` account) and return the outcome; `Reclaim` and `DecideReclaim` for the choice routes; deletion and purge call their hooks.
- **`internal/services/notifications`**: builders for `invitation.created` (decrypts at dispatch; obsolete when used, revoked, expired, `send_count` moved on or the tutor is no longer `active`), `relationship.ended` (recipient the student or the tutor, by `ended_by`; the email to the tutor names the student by the tutor's own card display name, escaped, never the current account name, and for a card created from a request the tutor-owned copy taken at accept time (D31); obsolete when reactivated) and `tutor.deleted` (obsolete when the tutor is no longer `deleted`); a builder whose subject row no longer exists (purged) marks the row `done`/`obsolete`; every email other than account and invitation emails is dropped for an unverified recipient, the other dispatch rules unchanged; one new HTML and text template pair with escaped user text.
- **`internal/worker`** jobs, hourly, batches through their partial indexes within the existing budget: `join_requests.expire` (200), `invitations.expire` (500, clears ciphertext), `reclaim.expire` (100 accounts; per account it locks the user row, re-checks `reclaim_until`, and drops every still-pending item per D27 whatever the account state; a deleted account never reaches it, because deletion already dropped its items).
- **`internal/api`**: audiences `tutor`, `tutor, unverified ok`, `student`, `student, unverified ok` beside the existing ones; role first (`404`), then state (`403 email_not_verified`); `POST /join-requests` and `POST /invitations/{token}/accept` (with a bearer) use `unverified ok` with a service role check answering `role_mismatch`; routers `tutoring_routes.go`, `group_routes.go`, `reclaim_routes.go`; `Cache-Control: no-store` on every response that carries an invitation link or accepts one.
- **`internal/schemas`**: tutor-facing `StudentCard`, `StudentCardSummary`, `TutorJoinRequest`, `Group`, `GroupSummary`; student-facing `MyTutor`, `SentJoinRequest`, `ReclaimList`; student structs never contain `display_name`, `notes`, the card email or other students.
- **Every backend change that alters the API** regenerates `frontend/src/lib/api/schema.ts` (`pnpm api:check` passes) and translates each new error code in `ru` and `en`.

### 4.5 Rules stated once

- **New-account acceptance**, in order: per-IP request slot → token usable and tutor active (else `invitation_invalid`) → validation → binding: a card email must equal the normalised sign-up email (the same normalisation as sign-up), and an ended card's invitation is bound to its account, so the new-account path is always a mismatch there; a mismatch is counted atomically and refused before any hashing → hash → insert-if-free → in one transaction: create the account `unverified`, consume the invitation, set the relationship `provisional`, choose the colour, queue the confirmation; for a taken email, change nothing and queue the duplicate notice. The invitation stays usable after a taken-email `202` (T-3 residual: a link holder who tries a taken email and later sees the link still valid learns nothing new, because the page offers sign-in either way). The page then signs in with the chosen password and on failure shows "check your email, or sign in to accept".
- **Signed-in acceptance**: role check and `email_not_verified` (K36) before any counter → token usable and tutor active → binding (the card email equals the account email, or the ended card's bound account is the caller), counting a mismatch → link: an existing non-discarded relationship with this tutor is kept (reactivated if ended) and the invited card becomes `discarded` (`merged`); a pending request from the same student becomes `accepted`.
- **Confirmation by the account's own browser**: provisional → active, held requests released (D7). **Reclaim**: provisional → `reclaim_pending`, held → `owner_choice_pending`, `reclaim_until` set; `GET /users/me/reclaim` lists them; `POST /users/me/reclaim` applies keep and drop (D4). A removal racing a confirmation or a reclaim locks the same user row and checks the state, so exactly one wins.
- **`reclaim_pending` transitions** (added to the transition tables of ADR-0014 and accounts.md):

  | From | To | Cause |
  |---|---|---|
  | `provisional` | `reclaim_pending` | Reclaim by the mailbox owner |
  | `reclaim_pending` | `active` | The owner keeps it while the tutor is `active`; or the owner accepts, signed in, an invitation of the same tutor, which counts as a keep and consumes that invitation |
  | `reclaim_pending` | `discarded` (`removed`) with a new `invited` card | The owner drops it; the owner keeps it while the tutor is not `active` (treated as a drop); `reclaim.expire` (D27); the tutor removes it (`POST /students/{id}/remove` accepts `reclaim_pending` as well as `provisional`, serialised on the student's user row); the student account is deleted or purged |
  | `reclaim_pending` | `discarded` (`tutor_deleted`), no new card | The tutor deletes their account; that tutor's held requests of the account expire |

  While `reclaim_pending` the link grants the account nothing: `GET /tutors` leaves it out, `POST /tutors/{id}/end` answers `404`, no group can take it, and the card's `account` is `null`. `MyTutor.state` therefore needs no new value.
- **Every transaction acting for a signed-in student** re-checks its session and the account state under the user row lock (4.3) before acting.
- **Ending** by either party: `ended`, `ended_by`, memberships deleted, open invitations revoked, `relationship.ended` to the other party with key `(relationship.ended, relationship, ended_at)`. A student's ending of a provisional link is a silent removal (D28).
- **Groups**: members are `active` relationships of the same tutor only; the D30 cap is counted under the tutor profile lock.
- **Linking a card without an email** never copies the account's email into `relationships.email`, so a dropped or removed link leaves no address on the new card.
- **Every link or reactivation path** (an invitation, an accepted join request, the owner's keep) revokes the card's other open invitation with `revoke_reason = linked` and clears its ciphertext, and a token links only while its card is `invited` or `ended`; every held or pending request from the same student to the same tutor becomes `accepted`.
- **Tutor deletion** revokes every open invitation of the tutor (`tutor_deleted`), including the regenerated links of ended cards.
- **Withdrawal** of a `provisional`, `reclaim_pending`, `active` or `ended` card answers `409 relationship_not_active`; a discarded card other than one this tutor withdrew is out of scope (`404`).
- **Refusal order:** role (`404`), then email confirmation (`403`, which depends only on the caller), both before the body is read (D32, D33), then parsing and the schema validation of the body (`400`, `422`, D34), then scope (`404`), then `role_mismatch` and state (`409`), then the service's remaining validation (`422`); the rows that deviate (for example `POST /join-requests`, which answers `422 tutor_code_invalid` before `409 already_linked`, and the new-account accept path) say so in api.md and accounts.md.
- **Code entry during a pending choice**: entering the code of a tutor with which the account has an `owner_choice_pending` request counts as a keep of that request (released under D7); with a `reclaim_pending` link it answers `409 already_linked` and changes nothing.

### 4.6 Observability

Owned by `services/tutoring` unless noted; fields `user_id`, `role`, `tutor_id`, `relationship_id` or `group_id`; never emails, names, notes, tokens or codes; access records name the route template `/api/v1/invitations/{token}`, never the token, also for malformed tokens.

### 4.7 Emails and limits

- `invitation.created` (required, high priority) to the card email: the tutor's budget is 10 per day in the first 7 days after sign-up and 50 after (`429`); 3 per recipient per day across tutors, and blocked or deleted recipient accounts, drop silently (D26).
- `relationship.ended` (required, normal) to the other party of the ending; not for provisional links (removal is silent); dropped for an unverified recipient.
- `tutor.deleted` (required, normal) to each confirmed student with an active relationship at deletion; key `(tutor.deleted, tutor, deleted_at, recipient)`.
- Code entry 10 per student per hour (held entries count); 50 visible pending requests per tutor; code regeneration 10 per tutor per day.

  - T-34: purge of cards, notes, invitations, requests, groups and discarded provisional links, batched tutor purge; no name snapshot is kept (D29).

Controls: one provisional link per account; no groups; no notification emails; the tutor can remove it; removal at 7 days; the mailbox owner's explicit keep or drop on reclaim. The session re-check under the user row lock (4.3) stops a squatter's in-flight request from acting after a reclaim, and a `reclaim_pending` link shows the tutor no account. The same-browser confirmation path, which keeps every link without a choice, relies on login-CSRF resistance: sign-in accepts only JSON with the exact `Origin`, and the refresh cookie is `SameSite=Strict`, so another site cannot sign a victim's browser in to the squatter's account before the victim confirms. ## 6. Risks, performance and the check budget

**Risks.**

- **Deadlocks** between accounts and tutoring transactions: one lock order (4.3), proved by the race tests.
- **Coupling** between `services/accounts` and `services/tutoring`: the hook interface keeps the import one-way; the architecture test enforces the layer table.
- **The reclaim page** adds a step after a password reset, which ends signed out today: the page appears after the next sign-in, and D27 bounds how long items wait.
- **Timing parity** of invitation and code sign-up: both paths hash; the taken-email path does fewer inserts, as on ordinary sign-up (T-3 residual).
- **Encryption key rotation** is unsupported in the MVP: a new key makes open links unreadable; the card offers Regenerate and the email builder marks such rows `dead` with `permanent` (T-19).
- **The owner's `backend/.env`** needs the new key before the next local API or worker start after S4; checks never depend on it.
- **Per-IP limits in end-to-end tests:** invitation and code sign-ups share the register counter (10 per hour); seeds send their own `X-Forwarded-For` through the trusted loopback proxy, as in slice 2.
- **Russian grammar:** state words and messages avoid names in case positions (K27).
- **Pilot assertions waiting for later slices** are marked `pending implementation`, never skipped silently.

**Performance.**

- Indexed lookups: code and token hash (unique), cards by `(tutor_id, state, created_at, id)`, relationships by `student_id`, visible pending requests by a partial index that also bounds the limit count (≤ 50 rows, taken under the tutor profile lock), expiry jobs through partial indexes, memberships by primary key and `relationship_id`, groups per card and first members per group in one batched query each.
- Lists are keyset paginated (default 30, maximum 100), except `GET /tutors` and `GET /groups` (D30); `q` scans one tutor's cards through the `tutor_id` index.
- Jobs and purges work in bounded batches within the existing job budget; the tutor purge is batched (500 rows per transaction).
- The slice review measures these query plans on 50,000 relationships, 100,000 join requests and 10,000 groups, serially on an idle machine.

**Check budget** (87 s of 600 s at the end of slice 2). Expected growth: about 110 Go integration tests (+15 to 25 s, parallel per-test databases) and about 30 Playwright tests in three browsers (+25 to 35 s), about 150 s in total. Kept down by seeding through the API, running API-only checks in Chromium only, sharing a seeded tutor per test file where tests do not mutate it, and keeping unit tests to the domain rules named above. Measured in F2 and F4 and recorded in `knowledge/analysis/`; above 180 s the suites are consolidated before more are added, never trimmed silently.
