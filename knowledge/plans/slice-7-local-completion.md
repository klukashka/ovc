## 1. Bounded recovery and creation coordinator

Create `internal/services/meetings` above repositories and the existing provider adapter. Add it to the package-layer map. Inject store, provider, business clock, monotonic timing, entropy and bound logger; no ambient globals or replacement HTTP transports. Extend the ledger through a new migration and precise repository operations, retaining the existing provenance and one-attempt semantics.

The admission contract is a global maximum of 4096 unsettled obligations, including open rooms and uncertain attempts. Admission refusal is explicit `503 service_unavailable` before an external Create. An atomic capacity reservation and ledger/business insertion must not leak slots on rollback; settlement releases a slot exactly once. Use a capacity row in a documented total order: existing scope/lesson locks, capacity, business meeting, ledger, outbox. Ledger-only cleanup that needs capacity locks capacity first and must never subsequently acquire business locks. Purge never acquires capacity after ledger. No provider wait holds a business transaction or pool connection.

Create dispatch is one Begin transition per immutable provider ID. Persist dispatch before calling Create; no automatic Create retry after cancellation, timeout or uncertain commit. Record a returned generation even if the request lost authorization or the lesson was removed, but never publish without a new scope/session/lesson check. Failed or stale creating business rows close and keep cleanup ownership. A still-young creating row may be awaited up to the existing 10-second deadline; a row older than one minute closes as the current video contract requires. Waiting uses explicit state observation under a deadline, not test sleeps. A request deadline is not proof that the provider stopped.

Recovery jobs scan every minute, at most 100 candidates per page and within a 20-second monotonic deadline including SQL waits. Persist a cursor with bounded compare-and-swap ownership, advance past failures and wrap after the end; eligibility acquired behind the cursor is revisited on the next traversal. No full-history scan per Tick. Queue work atomically with its ledger generation into the existing provider_call lane, at most four concurrent effects per process. Each effect retains the existing worker 30-second budget; cancellation/failed persistence never claims settlement. A future deployment's process count remains an explicit multiplicative concurrency limit, not a claimed global four-call cap.

Each cleanup cycle uses one stable outbox identity and at most the existing eight attempts/backoff. A durable result survives done/dead outbox retention. No scheduler recreates a spent cycle. A late definitive Create acknowledgement may request one additional final cycle, recorded atomically; it cannot generate unbounded cycles. Operator retries are explicit separate bounded cycles, not automatic retries hidden behind the scheduler.

Cleanup inspects by provider ID, and End always binds the exact observed/acknowledged generation. A changed generation is a safe conflict retained for operator inspection; do not end an unrelated replacement. Returned evidence plus successful End/absence can settle the acknowledged generation only when no future Create dispatch can occur. Prepared entries irrevocably revoked before dispatch may settle without a provider call. Started/uncertain and legacy-uncertain entries remain unresolved even after absence or ending a discovered room: remote quiescence is not proven. Their original provider identity stays discoverable, consumes admission capacity and survives purge. No fixed physical-erasure deadline is promised.

Settled ledger receipts can be removed after seven days by indexed batches of at most 100 within the same 20-second cleanup budget; never remove unresolved evidence. Application-generated provider IDs are fresh cryptographic UUIDs and never intentionally reused. Business meeting history keeps its existing ownership; deleting a receipt is not a business-row deletion. On migration backfill, count every existing ledger as unsettled; if above 4096 refuse new admission without dropping existing obligations.

Extend local `ovc-admin` with paginated status/inspect and explicit retry for a specified ledger/provider ID and expected evidence revision. No administrative HTTP route. Commands expose only safe typed status/counts, no names or credentials; unknown evidence cannot be force-settled or forgotten in this local stage. Repeated operator retries have new auditable identities and never mutate the original attempt proof. Capacity exhaustion caused by unresolved remote outcomes is an explicit availability limitation until a separately approved remote-quiescence recovery procedure exists.

Acceptance: real PG + SharedFake tests `TestMeetingCreateOwnership`, `TestMeetingRecoveryWorker`, `TestMeetingRecoveryCapacity`, `TestMeetingRecoveryCursor`, `TestMeetingRecoveryPurgeRace`, `TestMeetingRecoveryAdmin`. Prove crash/reconstruction, both commit/cancel orderings reachable through allowed injected sources and real locks, stale attempts, late acknowledgement, cap admission races/rollback, exactly-once capacity release, failed-item progress, finite cycles, outbox retention independence, receipt cleanup refusal for uncertainty and cancellation resource release. Real Tick uses the production composition. Negative controls must fail when late-publication protection, unresolved retention or generation binding is removed in an isolated copy.

## 2. Authorized lesson lifecycle API and process composition

Wire one shared provider namespace into API and worker through existing typed configuration; no independent in-memory instances in multiprocess tests. The fake remains the only activated provider until the remote probe. A navigable local video workflow requires the existing guarded test-control deployment: with fake configured but test control disabled, new video mutations refuse `503 service_unavailable` before state/provider mutation rather than returning an unusable synthetic or absent HTTP room; existing non-video features keep working. This is a local development demonstration, not permission to use test-control mode for real users. Introduce stable opaque per-lesson participant identifiers, separate from account identifiers, with a unique account/lesson mapping created only after authorization. Remove mappings with the owning lesson and participant account; no raw account ID goes to the provider. Migration and race tests prove uniqueness and cross-tutor isolation.

Implement existing planned routes `POST /lessons/{id}/join`, `/end`, `/restart-room`; add `room_open` and `embed_allowed` to typed lesson detail. Join returns `{url, embed_allowed}` with no-store; no credential enters the outbox, query cache, persistence or diagnostics. End returns the current authorized lesson detail and is idempotent. Restart returns the fresh join response only after the old generation is confirmed ended; otherwise return `503 provider_unavailable`, retain cleanup ownership and publish no replacement. Restart does not make uncertain revocation appear successful.

Apply the schedule join rule exactly: tutor in the window beginning ten minutes before start; student only while live with current active relationship, or provisional for one-to-one, and current group membership. Historical roster visibility never grants joining. Add a newly joining group student to the roster under existing relationship/group/lesson locks. Preserve current session rechecks after waits and before final publication/link release, including logout/reset/block/purge. Outside scope is 404; revoked session is 401; closed window is 409 outside_join_window; no usable student room is 409 lesson_not_live; provider failure is 503 provider_unavailable; admission capacity is 503 service_unavailable.

Use the existing persisted rate-limit infrastructure with a dedicated typed join purpose: 30 join attempts per account per minute, shared across that account's lessons and API instances. Malformed requests, initial authentication/scope refusals and the 429 itself consume no slot; after those checks reserve the slot in a committed admission step under the applicable user/profile/session checks, before window/live/provider work. Subsequent refusals and cancellation retain the charge. Return 429 rate_limited with Retry-After. Tutor End/Restart use the existing 120-per-hour lesson_writes_hour budget, after scoped lookup, including idempotent repeated End; students never consume a tutor's lesson-write slots. Document these new route rules and test exact boundaries, refusal charging and cross-instance races.

No locks span provider calls. Repeat authorization in a short final transaction; if it fails, discard the unreturned link and retain any cleanup responsibility. This does not claim revocation of earlier valid links: the existing accepted residual remains until the meeting ends. End, status-job expiry and tutor deletion close business access and queue cleanup atomically. Extend the existing status job to live lessons without weakening its checks or silently changing its separately recorded old scheduling-budget limitation. Publish entity.changed transactionally for room/lesson transitions.

Implement the planned unsigned ended-hint route: always 200, unknown/malformed keys reveal nothing, no direct end authority. Ignore rooms less than two minutes old or without a recorded join; debounce once per meeting/minute, check the provider through bounded worker work, recheck after two minutes at most three times if still present. Provider absence closes the matching room, not the lesson; a forged hint cannot close a room which still exists. Recording-ready stays absent. Aggregate admission uses a fixed bounded permit pool and silently declines scheduling while still returning 200 when saturated; never create an unbounded per-key memory table or replace the contracted 200 with a route-specific 429. No untrusted hint creates a room or renews a cleanup cycle.

Acceptance: real API/PG/worker `TestMeetingJoinAuthorization`, `TestMeetingJoinSessionRaces`, `TestMeetingConcurrentJoin`, `TestMeetingRestartRevocation`, `TestMeetingEndAndStatus`, `TestMeetingEndedHints`, `TestMeetingParticipantIdentity`, `TestMeetingRateAdmission`. Cover two tutors sharing one student, removed/ended/provisional/group cases, time boundaries, delayed provider completion after logout/purge, duplicate requests, restart old-link refusal and provider unavailability. Explicitly exercise simultaneous restart/restart, restart/join, end/join and restart/end in both observable lock/provider completion orders: at most one usable room, no reopen after End, no stale generation publication or cross-account link release, durable ownership of every refused late result. All source-path changes receive the applicable full backend checks and API schema/client regeneration.
