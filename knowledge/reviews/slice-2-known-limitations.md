# Slice 2 known limitations

Minor findings accepted during slice 2 instead of starting another review round ([ADR-0011](../../docs/decisions/0011-tiered-verification.md)). The slice review re-verifies each entry; each is fixed, accepted by the owner or carried into a named later slice.

| Finding | Severity | Change | Why it was accepted |
|---|---|---|---|
| The secret guard hook does not recognise a `HOME=…;` (or other already exported variable) assignment in an earlier statement of the same call, an exported `unset` function replaces the wrapper's own `unset` on bash 4 and later, inner interactive or login shells read startup files from the home directory, and the runner scan refuses some harmless commands | minor | Development tooling hardening after the slice 2 review | The hook and wrapper guard against accidental disclosure only; each is listed in the hook inventory in [AI development verification](ai-development-setup.md) |
| A fast series of language choices on a public page can reach the server out of order, so the account may keep an earlier choice until the page reloads | minor | Accessibility fixes after the slice 2 browser review | Needs a server answering out of order to test, and the fake HTTP layer this would need does not exist; the visible interface keeps the latest choice |
| `start` ends the session as unavailable after a failed start-up without checking the epoch | minor | Frontend session fixes after the slice 2 review | Unreachable today: nothing replaces the session while `start` runs |
| Recording and analytics behaviour is still described without an after-the-MVP note in `knowledge/specs/data-model.md` (the Recording entity), `knowledge/specs/files.md` (the recording quota), `knowledge/specs/accounts.md` (recordings after a relationship ends or an account is deleted), `docs/requirements/non-functional.md` (NFR-2.3, NFR-2.4, NFR-4.2 and the NFR-4.5 measurement condition) and the BigBlueButton research file's slice 7 probe list | minor | MVP scope: recordings and analytics after the MVP | The roadmap, the requirements and the video specification state the cut and the `record=false` control; the remaining mentions describe the later design and are corrected when recordings and analytics are planned |
