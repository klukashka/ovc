## Cancelled recovery wait lifetime

The recovery owns only its local wait, not the shared channel/history queries. An explicit cancellation promise races their completion; Cancel, starting another edit, replacing the recovery attempt and unmount settle that local promise. Its completion clears the pending wait reference and releases the old asynchronous editing frame, while edit-generation and account/channel guards still reject late results. Successful completion also settles and clears the local cancellation handle. No event listener, timer, polling loop or second cache is added, and no `cancelQueries` call can interrupt an unrelated parent's refetch. The pending function carries only the target message identifier rather than an extra original-message snapshot.

The additional offline cancellation regression covers reopening the same message and editing another message. It verifies that both shared reads remain paused after Cancel, the new editor owns focus and text, reconnection completes the shared reads, and the old conflict controls do not reappear.
