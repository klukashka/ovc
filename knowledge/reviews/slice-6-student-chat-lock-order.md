## Contract and implementation

Deletion and final purge now lock profiles named by every relationship and channel membership of the student, then all relationship rows, including discarded ones. After invitation and request work, they lock all affected group rows, ensure channels required by the remaining mirrors, and lock the complete union of audience channels and the student's membership channels in identifier order. This includes ended, discarded, provisional and reclaim-pending relationships and suspended members. The user row prevents new linking, while the profile locks prevent concurrent group membership changes from expanding the set. Later mirrors revisit already ensured channels; replacement invited cards create none. Final membership removal therefore obtains no additional channel lock after lesson effects.

Authorization predicates, recipients, lesson effects and the separate bounded message-purge batches are unchanged. The internal all-membership queries are deliberately included in the existing repository test's named mirror/purge exception list; they are not authorization readers. Existing structured membership, account, relationship and lesson diagnostics are preserved; no new content-bearing logging is added. The change meets the security trigger through deletion, purge and shared-channel availability (T-20/T-34), with negative scope and suspension coverage for T-21/T-44. The accounts contract and pending-owner-review threat-model wording accompany the implementation.

The earlier known-limitations rationale that user/profile locks serialize every competing transaction is invalid: a digest for another student sharing both groups holds neither the deleted student's user lock nor the tutors' profiles. The finding is reopened as major rather than retained as an accepted minor limitation.
