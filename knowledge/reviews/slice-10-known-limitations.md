Status: agent record, started 2026-11-16. Minor review findings and observed flaky tests of slice 10 changes that were accepted rather than fixed, and every failed merge gate of a slice 10 change that was gated again, whether a rerun or the predecessor of an after-fix gate, in the format of the numbered slices' files. Each entry is re-verified by the slice review of its sub-slice, which resolves it as fixed, accepted by the owner or carried into a named later sub-slice or slice. A row's sub-slice is the sub-slice whose slice review lists its change under the sub-slice rule of `AGENTS.md`, so a follow-up of one sub-slice's review that merged after that review started belongs to the next sub-slice. Each sub-slice's review record lists every passing gate of 600 s or more of its changes with the owner's answer and whether it came before the merge (added 2026-11-19 by the 10.1 slice review). The sub-slice column holds `10.1` to `10.5` or, for the stages named in `AGENTS.md` since 2026-11-23, `UI` (the interface rework stage), `FIN` (the finance stage), `BOOK` (the booking stage), `TASK` (the task tracker stage) and `PHONE` (the phone stage) (added 2026-11-23 with the interface rework plan's change U0, pending owner review).
