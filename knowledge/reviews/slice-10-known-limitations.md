# Slice 10 known limitations

Status: agent record, started 2026-11-16. Minor review findings and observed flaky tests of slice 10 changes that were accepted rather than fixed, and every failed merge gate of a slice 10 change that was gated again, whether a rerun or the predecessor of an after-fix gate, in the format of the numbered slices' files. Each entry is re-verified by the slice review of its sub-slice, which resolves it as fixed, accepted by the owner or carried into a named later sub-slice or slice. A row's sub-slice is the sub-slice whose slice review lists its change under the sub-slice rule of `AGENTS.md`, so a follow-up of one sub-slice's review that merged after that review started belongs to the next sub-slice. Each sub-slice's review record lists every passing gate of 600 s or more of its changes with the owner's answer and whether it came before the merge (added 2026-11-19 by the 10.1 slice review). The sub-slice column holds `10.1` to `10.5` or, for the stages named in `AGENTS.md` since 2026-11-23, `UI` (the interface rework stage), `FIN` (the finance stage), `BOOK` (the booking stage), `TASK` (the task tracker stage) and `PHONE` (the phone stage) (added 2026-11-23 with the interface rework plan's change U0, pending owner review). Since the change that makes CI the merge gate, a failed CI gate run that is gated again gets a `rerun` or `after-fix` row naming its run address, with the SHA-256 of each fetched log and the recorded lines quoted verbatim, and two further row kinds record a local gate that replaces the CI gate, each written with the owner's approval before that gate: `fallback`, when GitHub is unavailable, with the quoted trigger, every stalled or cancelled run's address, any use of the `main-ci` ruleset's bypass or its temporary disabling, and the catch-up run or the owner's decision after a failed catch-up; and `harness`, for a candidate that changes the CI harness, with the owner's approval and how the branch and `main` were pushed. The slice review re-verifies `fallback` and `harness` rows as it re-verifies `rerun` and `after-fix` rows ([ADR-0018](../../docs/decisions/0018-targeted-checks-and-merge-gate.md#ci-as-the-merge-gate); added 2026-11-27, pending owner review).
