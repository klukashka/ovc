## Gate reruns and failed gates before an after-fix gate

Every failed merge gate of a slice 10 change that was gated again, recorded in the change's own commit before the next gate. The kind follows [ADR-0018](../../docs/decisions/0018-targeted-checks-and-merge-gate.md): a `rerun` needs the owner's decision unless it is the one infrastructure rerun; an `after-fix` row carries, in its evidence column or in a record under `knowledge/` it links, for every failed assertion or check of that gate the quoted failure, the hunk that addresses it, the command, the before and after tree hashes with their exit statuses and, for an intermittent failure, the run and failure counts on each tree (at least 10 runs each, fewer only with the owner's approval), with every run made for the evidence recorded and none repeated, discarded or replaced. The development tooling reviewer of the sub-slice's slice review re-verifies every row, and each `after-fix` row against that evidence.
