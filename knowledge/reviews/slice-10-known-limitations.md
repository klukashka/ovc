## Gate reruns and failed gates before an after-fix gate

Every failed merge gate of a slice 10 change that was gated again, recorded in the change's own commit before the next gate. The kind follows [ADR-0018](../../docs/decisions/0018-targeted-checks-and-merge-gate.md): a `rerun` needs the owner's decision unless it is the one infrastructure rerun; every row holds its date, tested tree and base, the failing tests or checks and the owner's decision or the evidence, and an `after-fix` row carries, in its evidence column or in a record under `knowledge/` it links, for every failed test or check of that gate: the failure quoted from the gate's command log with the log's path and SHA-256, its match key (test title with project, or package and test name, plus the failing `file:line` or error kind; for a check, the command and finding), the hunk that addresses it, the command, the before and after tree hashes, and for every run its exit status and the output lines that name the test and its outcome; the after tree has 10 passing runs, and the before side is one reproducing run or a series fixed at 10 runs with at least one reproduction (another count only with the owner's approval before the first before run), with every run made for the evidence recorded and none repeated, discarded or replaced; [ADR-0018](../../docs/decisions/0018-targeted-checks-and-merge-gate.md)'s Failures bullet holds the full rule. The development tooling reviewer of the sub-slice's slice review re-verifies every row, and each `after-fix` row against that evidence.
