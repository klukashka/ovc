Each entry is re-verified by the slice review, which resolves it as fixed, accepted by the owner or carried into a named later slice.

## Flaky tests of the slice 8 merge gates

Recorded on 2026-11-11 by the change `fix/slice-8-flaky-tests`, which the owner approved on 2026-11-10 to fix the causes of every test that failed a slice 8 merge gate and passed alone, before the slice review. "Before" counts come from the gates and from repeated runs on `main` or a slice 8 change branch; "after" counts are repeated targeted runs (`--repeat-each`, `--trace=off`) of the change's tree on an otherwise idle machine. The slice review re-verifies each row.
