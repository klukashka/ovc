# Proposal NNNN: title

- Status: draft | proposed | deferred | accepted | rejected
- Reviewed: by the project owner, YYYY-MM-DD (required before the status leaves `draft`)
- Author: name
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Trigger: what has to happen for this proposal to be taken up; required for `deferred`
- Related: decision records, requirements, specifications

## Motivation

What problem this solves, for whom, and what happens if nothing is done. Cite requirements by identifier.

## Proposal

The change, concretely: which components, adapters, tables, routes, frames and screens change, and how. State what does not change.

## Alternatives

Each alternative with the reason it is not preferred.

## Plan

How the change would be delivered in slices, what each slice proves, and how it is tested with fakes instead of external services.

## Risks and open questions

What could invalidate the proposal and what is not yet known.

## Decision

Filled in when the status becomes `accepted` or `rejected`: the decision, the date, and links to the decision record and the specification changes.
